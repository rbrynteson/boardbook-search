535 POLICY - SERVICE ANIMALS IN SCHOOLS
I. PURPOSE
The purpose of this policy is to establish parameters for the use of service animals by students, employees, and visitors within school buildings and on school grounds.
II. GENERAL STATEMENT OF Policy
Individuals with disabilities shall be permitted to bring their service animals into school buildings or on school grounds in accordance with, and subject to, this policy.
III. DISSEMINATION OF POLICY
A. This policy shall appear in the staff handbook.
B. The school district will develop a method of discussing this policy with students and staff.
IV. PROCESS
The superintendent is directed to develop administrative procedures for Service Animals in School for the School District that meet the state and federal requirements established in law or rule.
