Minutes of Business Meeting
The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools
**Approved XXXX
A Business Meeting of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools (RAS) was held Monday, October 5, 2026, beginning at 7:00 p.m. in the Boardroom at the Robbinsdale Area Schools Education Service Center (ESC).
Welcome and Land Acknowledgement
Call to Order/Roll Call
Directors present: Bassett, Bowman, Brynteson, Evans-Becker, Hillenbrand, Long, and Wutoh (attended remotely); and Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent. Absent: None. There was a quorum, and the meeting was called to order.
Acceptance of the Agenda
MOTION: Bowman moved to approve the agenda, Brynteson seconded.
Motion prevailed with a unanimous roll call vote.
Rdale Proud: The Elementary Handbook Project
Bridget Dooley, Principal of Northport Elementary
Melissa Jackson, Principal of Forest Elementary
Jennifer Smith, Principal of FAIR School Pilgrim Lane
Shared the rationale and work on the development of a district handbook for elementary school families.
Special Report: Capital Projects Levy Report
Maureen Mullen, Director of Facilities and Operations
Joel VerDuin, Senior Director of Technology
Shared a progress report regarding projects using capital levy funds.
Consent Agenda
Consent Agenda items include administrative matters, personnel reports, and financial reports.
Long requested Item 6.A.1. Health and Dental Insurance Renewal be pulled for separate consideration/discussion.
MOTION: Brynteson moved approval of the Consent Agenda - excluding item removed for separate consideration,
Bassett seconded.
Voting in favor: Bassett, Bowman, Brynteson, Evans-Becker, Hillenbrand, Wutoh
Voting against: Long
Motion prevailed with a 6-1 roll call vote.
MOTION: Brynteson moved to approve the Health and Dental Insurance Renewal, Bowman seconded.
Voting in favor: Bassett, Bowman, Brynteson, Evans-Becker, Hillenbrand, Wutoh
Voting against: none
Abstaining from vote: Long (not okay with the increase in premiums)
Motion prevailed with a 6-0-6 roll call vote.
