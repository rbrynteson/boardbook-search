**Approved November 20, 2023
A Business Meeting of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools (RAS) was held Monday, November 6, 2023, beginning at 6:00 p.m. in the Boardroom at the Robbinsdale Area Schools Education Service Center. A recording of the meeting can be found at: https://www.rdale.org/discover/school-board under "Watch School Board Meeting
Webcasts."
Call to Order and Roll Call
Chair Bassett called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. Directors present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Sharon E.
Brooks, Dr. Greta Evans-Becker, Kim Holmes, Caroline Long, and John Vento; and Marti Voight, Interim
Superintendent. Director(s) absent: none. There was a quorum, and the meeting was called to order.
Acceptance of the Agenda
Chair Bassett asked for a motion to accept the Business Meeting agenda. Director Brooks moved to amend the Work
Session agenda to include discussion in regard to the role of Anne Becker of Becker Law LLC at the Governance
Policy Committee Meetings, and Director Holmes seconded the motion. Upon vote being taken thereon, the following voted in favor thereof: Directors Bassett, Bowman, Brooks, Holmes and Long. And the following voted against the same: Directors Evans-Becker and Vento. Said motion was declared duly passed.
Director Holmes made a motion to add an action item: norms for the Chair and dysfunctional agenda setting issues to the Business Meeting agenda. Director Vento requested to strike the request as an action item, instead making it item
4., a separate non-action item, moving Consent Agenda to item 5 - and all other agenda items after moving to accomm9date. Director Bowman seconded the motion. Upon vote being taken thereon, the following voted in. favor thereof: Directors Bassett, Bowman, Brooks, Evans-Becker, Holmes, Long and Vento. And the following voted against the same: none. Said motion was declared duly passed.
Chair B&ssett asked for a motion to accept the amended Business Meeting agenda. Director Evans-Becker moved to approve the agenda as amended, and Director Holmes seconded the motion. Upon vote being taken thereon, the following voted in favor thereof: Directors Bassett, Bowman, Brooks, Evans-Becker, Holmes, Long and Vento. And the following voted against the same: none. Said motion was declared duly passed.

Governance Policy Committee Members

Special Discussion: Norms for the Chair and Dysfunctional Agenda Setting Issues
Kim Holmes, School Board Vice Chair/Director
Director Holmes requested clarity of norms that the Board agrees to for the Chair as a facilitator, and for agenda setting - noting that decisions are to be made from the entire Board. Agenda setting meetings need to be scheduled, and last minute additions cannot keep happening. Discussion followed.
