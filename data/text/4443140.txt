3.A. Arts Showcase: "Junie B. Jones The Musical, Jr." by FAIR
ITEM:
Pilgrim Lane 5th Graders

PRESENTER:
Trudy Monette, Stages Theater Teacher
Janine Gagnon, FAIR Pilgrim Lane Music Teacher
Leslie Anderson, FAIR Pilgrim Lane 5th Grade Teacher
Tonight's Arts Showcase features students from FAIR Pilgrim Lane doing a number from their inaugural 5th grade capstone project - "Junie B. Jones The Musical, Jr."
One of the most special aspects of this capstone project is that while it was produced, designed, and directed by Stages Theatre Company professionals, the collaboration extended far beyond "Stages" class in the performance center. The benches and blocks onstage were designed and created by the 5th grade students in Art Class, alongside
Mrs. Kim Carroll. Music Direction was provided by FAIR Pilgrim Lane Music teacher,
Ms. Janine Gagnon over the course of the last 9 weeks. Ms. Trudy Monette, Stages
Teaching Artist in Residence at FAIR Pilgrim Lane, served as choreographer during
Stages class. This dedicated corps of fine and performing arts teachers came together in the most beautiful and collaborative way to send off this extremely special and worthy 5th grade class with a once in a lifetime, full-circle experience.
Of course, none of this was possible without the gracious and rigorous support of the
5th grade teaching team: Leslie Anderson, Jerid Schoeck, and Nathan Mathias, and the
FAIR Pilgrim Lane administrative staff and leadership: Randy Moberg, Jane Byrne, and
Anahi Urzua. Stages Theatre Company is continuously grateful to be a part of the FAIR
Pilgrim Lane community, and this project serves as a shining and perfect example of why we cherish our partnership with this incredible school community so much!
