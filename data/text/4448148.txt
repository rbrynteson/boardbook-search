ROBBINSDALE

Area Schools Minutes of Work Session
The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools

*"Reviewed December 18, 2023

A Work Session of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools (RAS) was held Monday, December 4, 2023 in the
Boardroom at the Robbinsdale Area Schools Education service Center. Complete agendas, reports, and presentations are available at the office and on our website. A recording of the meeting can be found at:
httos://www,rdale,org/discover/school-board under "Watch School Board Meeting Webcasts." Work session summary minutes are not approved by the School Board.

Director(s) present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Sharon E. Brooks, Dr. Greta Evans-Becker, Kim Holmes, Caroline
Long, and John Vento; and Marti Voight, Interim Superintendent. Director(s) absent: none.

Enrollment Projections

Marti Voight, Interim Superintendent

Virginia Verbrugge, Assistant Director of Finance

Dr. Robert Porter and Dr. James Sheehan

Dr. Porter and Dr. Sheehan presented regarding enrollment projections information for the next several years, with the data showing a continued slight decline in enrollment each year. Discussion followed.
