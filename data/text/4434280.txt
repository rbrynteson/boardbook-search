Executive Summary
● Aligned with MN READ Act and Science of Reading
● Reviewed K-5 ELA curricula with staff and community
● Recommendation: Adopt Wit & Wisdom and Functional
Phonics & Morphology (Gr 4-5)
● Implementation starts 2025-26 school year
4

Overview of Legislative Requirements
● READ Act requires adoption of evidence-based literacy resources
● Must cover phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, fluency, comprehension
● Use of state aid only for MDE-approved resources
● Robbinsdale already uses Bridge2Read for K-3 foundational skills
5

Curriculum Review Process
● Dec 2024-Apr 2025: Committee formed (K-5, EL, SPED, admin)
● March-April: Vendor meetings, demos, SPED & principal input
● May 2: Vendor fair for all staff and community
● Reviewed: EL Education and Wit & Wisdom
6

Stakeholder Feedback Overview
● Wit & Wisdom preferred by most stakeholder groups, including site administrators
● American Indian Education team endorsed W&W for cultural representation
● SPED staff preferred EL Education for scaffolding and supports
● Balanced preferences across general education and EL
teachers
7

Why Wit & Wisdom was Recommended
● Strong in comprehension, vocabulary, and language development
● Embedded supports for multilingual learners
● Rich academic materials and cross-curricular connections
● High equity focus; widely adopted across MN
● Positive district feedback despite planning demands
8

Considerations: EL Education
● Favored by SPED teams for structure and scaffolds
● Clear alignment with SPED and MN ELA standards
● Time-intensive for teachers; less effective in 3-5
● Effective with high fidelity implementation
9

Research and District Feedback
● Both programs have strong CAREI and district reviews
● Anoka-Hennepin & South St. Paul: High engagement with
W&W
● Shakopee & Bloomington: Strong writing and discourse with
W&W
● Concerns: Planning time, foundational supports

Preliminary 4-Year Quotes

● Functional Phonics & Morphology: $185,000 + $5,350 PD =
$190,350
● EL Education: $1,328,378 + $337,500 PD = $1,665,878

Conclusion and Next Steps
● Recommend: Wit & Wisdom (K-5) + Functional Phonics &
Morphology (Gr 4-5)
● Aligns with rigor, equity, and diverse representation
● Plan PD for K-5 staff and supports for SPED settings
● Ongoing implementation monitoring across subgroups
