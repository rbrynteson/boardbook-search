To: Board of Education
Date: April 2, 2024
Re: Additional information about operating referendums
Robbinsdale Area Schools is primarily funded by a combination of state funding and local property taxes. This includes an operating levy, approved by voters in 2014, that provides $24.8
million to the general fund. This levy is set to expire in 2024.
Robbinsdale Area Schools uses the operating referendum to fund the general expenditures of the district, including:
● Salaries ● Routine maintenance
● Staff benefits ● Office supplies
● Insurance ● Instructional materials and supplies
● Utilities ● Technology, equipment and repairs
New legislation brings school districts into alignment with city and county governments, and allows Minnesota school boards to automatically renew a previous operating referendum. This can be done one time only, either this year or next year.
Recommendation:
The School Board should pursue Option 1 with regard to renewing the district's operating levy.
