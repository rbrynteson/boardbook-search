To: School Board
From: Bo Powell, Director of Security
Date: December 18, 2023
Re: Student Safety Summit
On December 6, 2023 a school safety summit was held at Plymouth Community Center.
This was partly in response to a finding from the 2022 Minnesota Student Survey that 17% of
Robbinsdale students said they don't feel safe at their schools. Students from both Armstrong
High School and Cooper High School participated in the summit. Adults in attendance included:
the District Safety and Security Director, New Hope Chief of Police, Plymouth Chief of Police,
Plymouth Fire Chiefs, staff from the Department of Homeland Security and Department of Public
Safety, and a few staff from the two high schools. Twenty-five student participants completed a pre- and post-survey. Findings are summarized in this brief report.
All 25 students (100%) said they felt their voice was heard at the summit. Participants were asked to rate their time at the summit on a scale from one to five, with one being poor and five being excellent-the average score was 4.6. They were also asked about the extent to which the summit was helpful in building additional student ambassador relationships. Sixty-eight percent of respondents thought it helped build relationships between the two schools "a lot" and
71% thought it helped build relationships within their school "a lot".
