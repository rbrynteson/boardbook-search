Primary Election in 2026. The last Primary the school district conducted was in 2005, with nine candidates. Below is a summary of the election years from 2005 to now, along with the number of open 4-year term positions available versus the number of candidates for each year:

Attached is a sample resolution. To acquire an updated resolution, we would need to reach out to our attorney for an updated resolution and election packet. We have worked with Kennedy and
Graven in recent years, with Peter Martin listed on their website for school district elections.
The filing period for a Primary Election is May 19-June 2, 2026.
If 9 or more candidates file, then we would conduct the Primary Election on August 11, 2026 to reduce the candidates down to 8. If 8 or fewer file, then the Primary date in August would be N/A
and will proceed to the November 3, 2026 General Election.
The filing period for the General Election is July 14-28, 2026. The General Election is
November 3, 2026.
The estimated cost of an even year election is approximately $8,000-$10,000 depending on the number of ballots the City Clerks order. This includes legal publications, attorney fees, equipment programming and cost of ballots. We are charged for the space we consume on the ballot directly by Hennepin County Elections.
Election law changed in 2006, not requiring school districts to conduct a Primary
Election because of the additional cost, allowing the decision at the local level.
Also included as a reference, Minnesota Statute 205A.03 explaining the process to call a Primary
