GRANT OF PERMANENT EASEMENTS
INDEPENDENT SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 281, a Minnesota independent school district, hereinafter referred to as "Grantor", is the fee owner of certain real property located in the
City of Plymouth, County of Hennepin, State of Minnesota, that is legally described Exhibit A, and depicted on Exhibit C (the "Property").
Grantor, in consideration of One Dollar ($1.00) and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, does hereby grant and convey unto the CITY OF PLYMOUTH, a Minnesota municipal corporation ("City") as Grantee, its successors and assigns, forever, the following:
Permanent Easements:
A. ROW Easement. A perpetual and permanent easement for public right of way purposes ("Easement 1") over, on, across, under, and through that portion of the Property that is legally described on Exhibit B and depicted on Exhibit C ("Easement Area 1"). Easement 1
includes the right of the City, its contractors, agents, servants, and assigns, to enter upon Easement
Area 1 at all reasonable times to construct, reconstruct, inspect, repair, and maintain Easement 1
and Easement Area 1, together with the right to grade, level, fill, drain, pave, and excavate

Easement Area 1, and the further right to remove trees, bushes, undergrowth, and other obstructions interfering with the location, construction, and maintenance of Easement 1 and

B. Trail and Drainage and Utility Easement. A perpetual and permanent easement for trail and drainage and utility purposes ("Easement 2") over, on, across, under, and through that portion of the Property that is legally described on Exhibit B and depicted on Exhibit C
("Easement Area 2"). Easement 2 includes the right of the City, its contractors, agents, servants, and assigns, to enter upon Easement Area 2 at all reasonable times to construct, reconstruct, inspect, repair, and maintain Easement 2 and Easement Area 2, together with the right to grade, level, fill, drain, pave, and excavate Easement Area 2, and the further right to remove trees, bushes, undergrowth, and other obstructions interfering with the location, construction, and maintenance of said Easement 2 and Easement Area 2.
The above named Grantor, for itself, its successors, and assigns, does covenant with the
City, its successors and assigns, that it is well seized in fee title of Easement Area 1 and 2; that it has the sole right to grant and convey the Permanent Easements to the City; that there are no unrecorded interests in Easement Area 1 or 2; and it will indemnify and hold the City harmless for any breach of the foregoing covenants.
Exhibit A, Exhibit B, and Exhibit C are attached to this Grant of Permanent Easements, and are incorporated into and made part of the same.
