ROBBINSDALE

Area Schools Minutes of Closed Session
The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools

*"Approved July 22, 2024

A Closed Session of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools, ISD 281, was held Monday,
July 8, 2024, beginning at 9:50 p.m. in the Superintendent's Office at the Robbinsdale Area Schools Education

Service Center, 4148 Winnetka Avenue North, New Hope, Minnesota.

1. Call to Order and Roll Call
Chair ReNae Bowman called the meeting to order at 9:50 p.m.
Directors present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Sharon E. Brooks, Dr. Greta Evans-Becker, Kim
Holmes, Caroline Long and John Vento

Directors absent: None

Other attendees: Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent and Amy O'Hern, Executive Director of Human
Resources

2. School Board

A. Closed Session pursuant of Minnesota Statute 13D.03, Labor Negotiations Strategy
Chair Bowman announced that in accordance with Minnesota Statute 13D.03, the meeting is closed to the public for the purpose of labor negotiations strategy. Keeping with District Administrative Policy 205, the proceedings were recorded.

3. Adjournment
Chair Bowman adjourned the meeting at 10:36 p.m.
