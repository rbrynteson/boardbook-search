From: Amy O'Hern, Executive Director of Human Resources

RE: Fund Transfer
During the Minnesota Department of Education (MDE) resource review, a $40.95 expenditure on pizza and drinks was flagged as an unallowable expense. This expense occurred in September of
2023, when there was a sewage backup in the Armstrong kitchen. The district bought pizza for nutrition services staff who cleaned up the kitchen, rather than having them go to another site for lunch.
It was approved as an 01 expense, but an 02 code was submitted. Per MDE, we need to provide the posted journal entry demonstrating that the total amount of unallowable costs has been restored to the 02 Fund, and documentation to support this amount. This entry must be recorded in the current fiscal year, and allocated to Source Code 649. Attached you will find the appropriate journal entry form.
