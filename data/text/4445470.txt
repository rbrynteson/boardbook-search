Re: Resignation of Financial Advisory Council Member Ukee Dozier

Council indicating timing and other circumstances aren't conducive at this time to follow through with the FAC commitment. Ukee's appointed term was July 1, 2024-June 30, 2027.

Lankford and Nicole Sandback. The School Board will need to appoint three (3) new memberships for the term of July 1, 2025-June 30, 2028. Ukee's resigned term will be by appointment for
July 1, 2025-June 30, 2027
Recommendation: Accept Ukee Dozier resignation from the Financial Advisory Council effective
March 17, 2025.
