**Approved April 22, 2025
A Business Meeting of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools (RAS) was held Monday, April 7, 2025, beginning at 6:00 p.m. in the Boardroom at the Robbinsdale Area Schools Education Service Center (ESC). A recording of the meeting can be found at: https·/fwww rdale org/djscoyer/school-board under "Watch School Board Meeting Webcasts."
Call to Order and Roll Call
Directors present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Dr. Greta Evans-Becker, Aviva Hillenbrand, Kim Holmes, and
Caroline Long; and Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent. Director(s) absent: none. There was a quorum; and Chair
Evans-Becker called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.
Approval of the Agenda
MOTION: Director Holmes moved approval of the agenda, and Director Hillenbrand seconded the motion.
Motion prevailed with a 6-0 voice vote.
Ceremonial Oath of Office for Newly-Appointed School Board Director
Chair Evans-Becker presented the newly-elected School Board Director, Dr. Kenneth Wutoh. The ceremonial oath of office was recited by Dr. Wutoh. Director Wutoh then affirmed the Conflict of Interest Administrative Procedure -
stating his wife works in the district as a teacher, so he will abstain from voting on the teacher contract.
Rdale Proud - Showcase: Robbinsdale Middle School (RMS) - Robins Break
Shirrie Jackson, Principal at RMS
Colleen Kennedy, Assistant Principal
Noelle Nelson, Administrative Intern
Ms. Jackson, Ms. Kennedy, and Ms. Nelson presented information regarding Robins Break at RMS, which began at
RMS January 13, 2025 and will continue through the end of the school year. These occasional movement breaks occur in the gym A/B or outside the last 10 minutes of lunch. Movement opportunities include: basketball, walking, volleyball, soccer, double dutch, etc. To participate, the students must keep moving.
Superintendent's Report
Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent
Superintendent Staloch reminded everyone of the Rdale mission to inspire and educate all learners to develop their unique potential and positively contribute to their community, then shared some of the highlights since the last meeting, including:
•
Rdale's Adventure Club program was recently honored by readers of the Sun Post as the #1 summer camp -
offering a safe, fun, and enriching environment staffed by caring professionals who foster a sense of belonging and adventure - an exciting recognition of the program's ongoing commitment to students and families.
•
Sixth grade students at Sandburg Middle School (SMS) showcased their science projects at their
Science/Imagination Fair on March 21, 2025. T he students used recycled materials to create projects focused on reducing pollution and litter.
•
District middle school students successfully represented their schools at the MNUDL City Championship on
March 26, 2025 in the following divisions:
o Rookie Division:
■ Shy Watkins and Mecko Mix from RMS - 1st place as a team
■
Violet Bruech from Plymouth Middle School (PMS) - 1st place Rookie Individual Speaker Award
■ Four students placed in the top ten of Rookie Individual Speaker Awards o Novice Division:
■ Chloe Grant and Ally Trei from FAIR Crystal - 2nd place as a team
•
CHS was honored to welcome U.S. Congresswoman llhan Omar for an engaging Q&A session with students, where Rep. Omar encouraged students to engage in their community and feel confident in themselves.
•
Reimagine Rdale Vision 2030 has reached 10,295 engagement touchpoints - 20% of our 50,000 goal - continuing engagement meetings with middle school students, and focus groups with students and staff coming soon.

Consent Agenda
Consent Agenda items are considered routine in nature and are enacted by one motion. There will be no separate discussion of these items unless a Board member so requests, in which the item will be removed as a Consent
Agenda item, and addressed. Consent Agenda items include administrative, personnel matters, and financial matters.
Director Holmes made the request to pull the Licensed Staff Personnel Report for further discussion.
MOTION: Director Hillenbrand moved approval of the consent agenda - excluding the Licensed Staff Personnel
Report, Director Wutoh seconded the motion.
