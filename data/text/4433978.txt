To: Board Chair, School Board Directors, and Superintendent Staloch
From: Joel Mehring, Assistant Director of Technology
Date: May 19, 2025
Re: Data Center Infrastructure Refresh Request for Approval
This memo requests the school board's approval for a critical refresh of the district's data center infrastructure, including hardware, storage, backup, and cyber vault solutions. The current infrastructure, purchased in 2020, is nearing the end of its optimal lifespan.
The Technology Department has proactively assessed our current data center hardware and software, which are approximately five years old. Industry best practices suggest a 3-5 year refresh cycle for critical infrastructure to maintain performance, reliability, and security. We engaged with two major vendors, Dell
Technologies (partnering with our local reseller Cyber Advisors) and Hewlett-Packard Enterprise (HPE, partnering with another valued local reseller, High Point Networks), to explore viable refresh options.
Continuing to maintain the aging infrastructure would incur increasing annual support costs without the benefit of modern performance and security features. Investing in new hardware with long-term maintenance and support provides a more cost-effective and strategic approach. Furthermore, repurposing the existing servers to upgrade our surveillance camera infrastructure offers a valuable secondary benefit, enhancing overall school safety. This initiative will be funded through the technology levy.
To ensure a comprehensive and objective selection process, we employed an evaluation matrix based on key factors: total cost of ownership (including potential additional costs), features/ease of use, and vendor experience and support. High Point Networks/HPE scored 4.15 out of 5, while Cyber Advisors/Dell scored
3.55 out of 5. The higher score for High Points Networks/HPE reflects a strong alignment with our needs across this critical area, particularly in terms of cost effectiveness and the comprehensive nature of the proposed solution.
The proposed solution from High Point Networks/HPE meets all our current technical requirements and provides a scalable platform for future growth. Importantly, it also represents the lowest overall cost to the
District.
