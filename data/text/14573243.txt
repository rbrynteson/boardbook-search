District Enrollment Projection Variance
& Comprehensive Trend Analysis Report
1. Executive Summary & Policy Corrections
Between October 2024 (10,253 students) and October 2026 (9,137 students), district K-12
enrollment declined by 1,116 students (-10.88%). Standard cohort-survival demographic models projected an average 2-year decline of 418 students (-4.08%), leaving an unexplained negative variance of -698 students.
To accurately isolate non-demographic causes from underlying population shifts, this document incorporates three critical policy and operational corrections based on actual district implementation:
● Phased Minneapolis Busing Removal (2025-26 vs. 2026-27):
○ FY26 Phase 1: The initial partial reduction of Minneapolis transportation in
2025-2026 had very minimal impact on district enrollment.
○ FY27 Phase 2: The total elimination of all remaining Minneapolis busing in
2026-2027 generated a major structural drop, accounting for an estimated -220 to
-260 open-enrolled Minneapolis students who transitioned out of the district.
● ICE Enforcement Operations Timing: ICE enforcement activity took place in early 2026; however, no pupil reduction occurred in January 2026 as a result. Instead, the enforcement impact manifested later during the spring months (February through April
2026), accounting for a portion of the 276-student drop observed between October 1,
2025 and June 1, 2026.
● Building Closures & Consolidations (2026-2027): School consolidations (e.g., middle school shifts and elementary site mergers for FY27) generated transitional friction, with an estimated accounting for approximately -120 to -150 students in transition/open-enrollment loss.

3. Quantitative Impact Analysis of Specific Factors
3.2 ICE Enforcement Impact Analysis (Spring 2026)
Monthly district enrollment records verify that no drop occurred in January 2026. Enrollment remained steady through January before declining in February, March, and April 2026. This spring drop accounts for approximately 140 of the 276 mid-year departures between October
1, 2025 (9,917) and June 1, 2026 (9,641).

3.2 Building Closure & Realignment Friction (2026-2027)
Consolidating middle school operations and elementary sites for the 2026-2027 school year created a transitional open-enrollment leak of ~120 to 150 students as reassigned families evaluated alternative options during the transition period.
