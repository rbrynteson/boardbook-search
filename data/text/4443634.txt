ITEM:
Virginia Verbrugge, Assistant Director of Finance
PRESENTER:
Ukee Dozier from School Management Services LLC
Description:
Minnesota Statute 275.065, which was revised in 2009, requires that a district must hold a public hearing to discuss the proposed property tax levy for taxes payable in a subsequent year as well as the district's current year adopted budget. The district is also required to adopt proposed levy and allow for public comment and questioning immediately after presenting.
