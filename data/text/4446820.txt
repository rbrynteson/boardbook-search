Expand opportunities for students to connect with district leaders
281 Youth Council
● The Robbinsdale Area Schools Youth Council (281YC) brings student leaders together to drive change at the district level. This council empowers students to develop leadership skills, elevate youth voice, and address issues that directly impact students throughout Robbinsdale
Area Schools. Members work alongside district leaders to design and support youth-centered systems change while serving as advocates and leaders in their school communities.
Student Safety Squad
● The Student Safety Squad is a group of students who help make school a safe, welcoming, and supportive place. Building on ideas from the district's Student Safety Summit, students share their perspectives on safety, well-being, and belonging. Members help start conversations, give feedback to adults, and support actions that make students feel heard, included, and supported at school.

Expand opportunities for students to connect with district leaders
Superintendent Student Advisory Council
● The Superintendent Student Advisory Council provides students with a direct opportunity to advise the Superintendent and district leadership on issues affecting learning, school climate, and the overall student experience. Council members represent student voices from across the district, share feedback, discuss district initiatives, and offer ideas to improve schools. This group empowers students to engage in meaningful dialogue with decision-makers and gain insight into district-level leadership.

Establish Anchor
The Rdale Anchor Partner Network is about coming together to better serve the students and families of Robbinsdale Area Schools. The
District recognizes that strong, lasting partnerships are the foundation of thriving circle schools and communities. By creating a of "anchor partners," we can combine our strengths, share insights, and bring more opportunities to the families we serve.
Anchor partners are more than collaborators; they are trusted allies who bring resources, expertise, and energy to help our students feel supported both in and out of succeed and school.
