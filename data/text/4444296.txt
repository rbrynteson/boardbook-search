To: Members of the School Board
From: Amy O'Hern, Executive Director of Human Resources
Date: May 20, 2024
Re: Tentative Agreement - Cabinet
RECOMMENDATION:
The District Administration is recommending the approval of a two-year Cabinet Terms and
Conditions with effective dates of July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2025. The following items have been discussed.
Salary increase of 3% each year.
Increase 403b match from $2500 to $5000.
Remove $100 monthly phone allowance from terms and conditions.
Remove $2000 professional development/technology stipend from terms and conditions.
