Silver Platter Day is a 24-year Cooper tradition where IB Full Diploma candidates present their completed Extended Essay to their supervisor.
The Extended Essay is a 4,000 word research project which students have worked on for the past
12 months. Completing this essay is a huge accomplishment on their path to earning the full IB
diploma.
