Land Acknowledgement
We acknowledge Robbinsdale Area Schools is located on the homelands of the Dakota and Ojibwe people.
We recognize the painful history of genocide and forced assimilation of the Indigenous inhabitants of this land.
We honor and respect the many Indigenous peoples who live on and hold sacred these lands, and we stand with members of these
Nations to fight injustice in all of its forms.
We uphold the preservation of Dakota and Ojibwe languages, land based education, and tribal sovereignty.

Bridge2Read
Teaches early reading phonic skills that follow the Science of Reading research for systematic and explicit teaching of mastery reading skills that lead to reading comprehension and increased reading growth in testing scores.

Bridge2Read
Implementation
● Approved Phonics Skill Program from MDE's updated Read Act 2024.
● Introduced in Sonnesyn Grade 3 in Fall 2022 with coaching support through a 3-year grant by the
Ciresi Walburn Foundation.
● Implemented in all K-5 classrooms Fall 2023 with coaching support. Continued implementation with
STARS/WAVE program as well as K-3 classrooms with coaching support.

Bridge2Read Components
● Phonemic Awareness
● Letter/Digraph Sounds
● Sentence Reading
● Blending
● Reading Words
● Dictation
● Accuracy and Automaticity
