Mission
The mission of Robbinsdale Area
Schools is to inspire and educate all learners to develop their unique potential and positively contribute to their community.

Supporting Our Community
Rdale remains focused on student safety, belonging, and learning.
We continue to follow safety protocols, support families with resources, and provide flexibility - including temporary online learning options when needed.
Questions should be directed to your school principal, and resources are available at rdale.org → Community → All Are
Welcome: Immigration Resources.

All Invited: Black History Celebration
Rdale will host its annual Black History
Month Celebration from 5:30-7:30 p.m. on
Friday, Feb. 20 at Robbinsdale Middle
School.
The event brings our community together to honor the past, celebrate the present, and inspire the future.

Triple 'A' Nominees
Four Rdale seniors - Natalie Gustafson and
Anders Hassis (Armstrong), and Niko Her and Keziah Omoke (Cooper) - have been nominated for the MSHSL Triple 'A' Award.
The award recognizes excellence in
Academics, Arts, and Athletics across
Minnesota high schools.

Quiz Bowl Success
The Armstrong Quiz Bowl Team qualified for the Minnesota High School Quiz Bowl
League Playoffs on Feb. 12.
Junior Evie Johnson was named the third-highest individual scorer in the West
Division.
Earlier this year, the team even tested their skills in a friendly match against our School
Board.

Rdale Olympian
Lucinda Anderson, Armstrong Class of 2019, will make her Olympic debut Feb. 8 in biathlon at the 2026 Winter Games in
Milano-Cortina.
A former state champion Nordic skier at
Armstrong, Anderson's journey from Rdale classrooms to the world stage is an incredible source of pride.

ExCEL Award Leaders
Wyatt Dean and Kyllander Nelson, juniors at Cooper High School, have been named
MSHSL ExCEL Award recipients - selected from 348 nominees statewide.
The award honors students who demonstrate excellence in community service, education, and leadership.

State Teacher of the Year Nominee
Markus Hahn, band teacher at Cooper High
School, has been named a candidate for
Minnesota's 2026 Teacher of the Year, a statewide program recognizing excellence in education.
His selection continues Rdale's strong tradition of educators earning statewide recognition and reflects the impact our teachers have on students every day.

Thank you!
