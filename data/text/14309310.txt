To: School Board Members and Superintendent
From: Kristen Hoheisel - Chief Financial Officer
Date: September 1, 2026
Re: Workers Compensation Renewal - Consent Agenda
RECOMMENDATION:
It is recommended that we renew our Workers' Compensation Insurance Policy for the policy year October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027 with SFM Insurance
Company for the estimated annual premium of $831,974.
DISCUSSION:
The renewal premium is a decrease of $65,758 from the previous year premium.
The Experience Modification Factor decreased from 1.12 to 1.05, the District received a premium discount which is reflected in the renewal decrease. The Experience
Modification Factor is a term used in the American insurance business and more specifically in workers' compensation insurance. It is the adjustment of annual premium based on previous loss experience. Usually three years of loss experience are used to determine the experience modifier for a workers' compensation policy.
Overall total cost before adjustments dropped from $1,008,560 to $923,020.
