ROBB! NSDALE Minutes of Work Session

Area Schools The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools

"Reviewed April 17, 2023

A Work Session of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools was held Tuesday, April 4, 2023. Complete agendas, reports, and presentations are available at the office and on our website. Work session summary minutes are not approved by the School Board.

Director(s) present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Sharon Brooks Green, Greta Evans-Becker, Kim Holmes, Caroline
Long, and John Vento; and David Engstrom, Superintendent. Director(s) absent: none.

SY24 Bell Schedule Adjustment Proposal/Transportation Opt-In

Carrie Johnson, Director of Transportation presented a proposed bell schedule adjustment, and information regarding the upcoming transportation opt-in process. After discussion, it was determined that the bell schedule would not be changed for SY24.

Board Topics

A. Board Priorities
Due to the length of both of the evening's meetings, it was decided to add a Special Work Session on Tuesday,
April 11, 2023 at 6:30 p.m. to discuss safety, security, climate and culture. These items represent the most critical areas on which the School Board wants to focus.

B. Discussion of Policy 214: Out-of-State Travel by School Board Members
Director Brooks Green brought her concerns about this policy forward, but this topic was tabled for a later date.
