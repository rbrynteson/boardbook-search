Re: Donation of School Buses-Non Action
The School Board is asked to review the attached "Donation Agreement" that will be presented to the School Board for approval on August 21, 2023.
We are seeking approval from the School Board to donate obsolete buses to non-profits. The buses are 74 passenger and are 2007 and 2008 models. They were previously placed on Public
Surplus to sell, but they did not receive any bids. These buses do not run and will be used for training purposes. By donating to the non-profits, it allows the entities opportunities to provide training services to their staff. The five (5) buses will be donated to the following entities:
● Eden Prairie Police Department
● Crystal Police Department
● Hennepin County Sheriff
● MN State Patrol
● Chaska Fire Department
