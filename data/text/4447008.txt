To: School Board Members and Superintendent Dr. Teri Staloch
From: Maureen Mullen, Director of Facilities and Operations
Date: May 18, 2026
Re: RSI Secure Entry Improvements Contract Award Recommendation
Background
Secure entry improvements were designed for the Robbinsdale Spanish Immersion Elementary
School (RSI) by OPN Architects and KFI Engineering. The projected cost for the designed scope fell below the $175,000 threshold for a formal bid process.
Bid details
On Thursday, April 30, we received quotes for RSI secure entry improvements from three companies: Brennan Construction of MN, Inc., Versacon, Inc., and BCI Construction. The low quote for the project was received from Brennan Construction of MN, Inc.
Recommendation
After reviewing the quotes, we recommend that the Robbinsdale Area Schools Board of
Education award the general contract for RSI Secure Entry Improvements to Brennan
Construction of MN, Inc. for the sum of $103,000.00
