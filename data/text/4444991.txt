To: School Board Members and Superintendent Staloch
From: Anthony Williams, Executive Director Community Education,
Athletics, and Activities
Date: December 02, 2024
Re: Action - Armstrong/Cooper Wings Swim Cooperative with Northwest
Passage High School for 2024-2025
Description:
AC WIngs Boys Swim began the 2023-2024 school year as a Co-op between Robbinsdale
Armstrong and Robbinsdale Cooper High Schools. We are requesting entering into an additional cooperative sponsorship for Boys Swim, adding Northwest Passage High School (NPHS)
beginning in the 2024-2025 school year.
Our current Boys AC Swim and Dive roster numbers from both Armstrong and Cooper High
Schools have decreased over the past few years. Last year, the Armstrong and Cooper Co-op had
13 swimmers in which 7 were seniors and graduated in 2024. This year, for the 2024-2025 school year, we currently have 12 registrations with potentially 4 more interested in joining, giving us a potential total of 16.
Entering into a cooperative sponsorship with Northwest Passage High School will allow us the opportunity to first service a student that once swam for us and lives in district 281 while attending
NPHS. Secondly, this partnership will also allow us to allow students from NPHS see the amazing culture and climate that 281 provides for our students. Adding students to our current Co-op aligns with our original purpose of the Co-op to grow the program to remain competitive with our surrounding schools. The understanding is that the team will continue to serve as AC Wings Boys
Swim as a brand.
The Boys Swim team will continue to be managed by the Cooper Activities Department and will compete in the Tri-Metro Conference.
Resolution:
Requesting approval of Cooperative Sponsorship Application for Boys Swim for Armstrong and
Cooper High Schools.
