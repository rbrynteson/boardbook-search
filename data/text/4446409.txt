RESOLUTION APPOINTING A BOARD MEMBER
WHEREAS, Minnesota Statutes section 123B.09, subdivision 3, states that a vacancy on the School Board occurs when a member resigns;
WHEREAS, School Board member Kim Holmes resigned from her seat on the Board effective October 6, 2025;
WHEREAS, Minnesota Statutes section 123B.09, subdivision 5b, states that any vacancy on a school board of an independent school district must be filled by board appointment at a regular or special meeting;
WHEREAS, School Board Policy 215 governs the procedure for filling vacancies,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the School Board of Independent School
District No. 281, State of Minnesota, as follows:
1. Pursuant to Minnesota Statutes, Section 123B.09, Subd. 5b, Richard "DJ"
Brynteson is hereby appointed to fill the vacancy created by the resignation of
Board Member Kim Holmes.
2. Because fewer than two years remain in the unexpired term, by statute there will be no special election, and Mr. Brynteson will serve the remainder of the unexpired term and until a successor is elected and qualifies.
3. The appointment shall be effective thirty (30) days after the adoption of this resolution as provided in Section 123B.09, subd. 5b.
Moved by: Evans-Becker, Seconded by: Bassett
The following voted in favor:
Bassett, Bowman, Evans-Becker, Hillenbrand, Long, Wutoh
The following voted against: None
WHEREUPON the resolution was declared duly passed and adopted.
