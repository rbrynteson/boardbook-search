General Overview
As the 2022-23 school year progressed, it was clear the current start times were challenging for our secondary students. They didn't have enough time to transition from their bus, to breakfast, then to the classroom.
School Year 2023-2024 Change to Bell Schedule Adjustments:
● adjust elementary school start times 15 minutes earlier, to 7:30 a.m.
● move high school start times 5 minutes earlier to 8:30 a.m.
● move middle school start times 10 minutes later, to 9:30 a.m.
These changes will allow transportation to have the appropriate time between tiers, providing drivers more time between routes, and students more time after drop off, which will prevent tardiness and truancies.
Dismissal times would be adjusted correspondingly.

Specifics
● Magnet Schools will stay at their current times
