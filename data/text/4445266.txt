2024-25 Priority Work A3
Priority Work
1. Increase opportunities for applied career experiences
2. Integrate career exploration within the PK-12 curriculum
3. Define the essential characteristics of the future graduate in collaboration with our community, and align learning opportunities for all students

Key Performance Indicators A3
● All 6-12 students have access to a career and college exploration curriculum/platform
● All staff will have access to utilize the college and career lesson planning guides and curriculum
● Career Pathways aligned to student choice, community needs and industry standards.

Deliverables for 2024-25 A3
● Build out RPathways experiences and exploration to include courses, workstudy, internships, and apprenticeships
● Increase CTE (Career and Technical
Education) and college credit course offerings
● Create an advisory committee
● Xello training (college and career readiness platform) for 6-12 school counselors and advisory coordinators
