To: Members of the School Board
From: Amy O'Hern, Executive Director of Human Resources
Date: May 20, 2024
Re: Tentative Agreement - Program Directors
RECOMMENDATION:
The District Administration is recommending the approval of a two-year Program Director Terms and Conditions with effective dates of July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2025. The following items have been discussed.
Salary increase year one 3% and year two 4.3%
Cafeteria increase of $100 in year two
Increase vacation buy back to 15 days
Remove $1000 technology stipend from terms and conditions.
Remove $60 monthly phone allowance from terms and conditions.
