Executive Summary:
Graduation Rates 2024
● Overall Decline Masking Subgroup Growth:
○ Four-year graduation rate decreased to 78.6 percent which is 2.7 percentage points lower than in 2023
○ Five-year graduation rate decreased to 84.3 percent which is 2.7 percentage points lower than in 2023
○ Seven-year graduation rate increased to 88.9 percent which is 0.2
percentage points higher than in 2023
○ Graduation rates increased for American Indian students from 57.7 to 69.0
percent and Black/African American students from 73.9 to 77.0 percent
● Graduation Rate Changes per School
○ AHS: from 91.6 percent in 2023 to 91.1 percent in 2024
○ CHS: from 86.0 percent in 2023 to 85.5 percent in 2024
○ RA: from 52.1 percent in 2023 to 50.5 percent in 2024
○ RVA: from 60.7 percent in 2023 to 53.3 percent in 2024

Strategies to Improve
Graduation Rates
● AHS and CHS have fully implemented the BARR program (Building Assets
Reducing Risk) at the 9th-grade level
● Highview has implemented a multiple-cycle model whereby students can attain credits at an accelerated rate
● RVA has implemented a system that requires full-time teachers who are dedicated only to RVA students
● Each high school has incorporated blended learning into its curriculum
● Rdale offers both extended day and extended year credit recovery - additionally,
Armstrong and Cooper offer a credit completion program at the end of
Semester I
● Armstrong has Falcon Flex Time and Cooper's Hawk Lunch and Learn
● All schools have a robust multi-tiered system of supports (MTSS) program
