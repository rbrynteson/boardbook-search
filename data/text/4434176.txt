To: Members of the School Board, Superintendent Staloch
From: Amy O'Hern, Executive Director of Human Resources
Date: July 7, 2025
Re: 2025-26 Calendar Revisions
RECOMMENDATION:
The district administration is recommending changing two dates in the 2025-2026 school year calendar. These recommendations have been discussed with the calendar committee.
The calendar committee consists of district teachers, administrators, RFT president and department directors. The recommendations were also shared with the district learning leaders.
Our current 2025-26 school year calendar has a compensatory day on May 8, 2026. However, the
Advanced Placement (AP) exams are administered over two weeks in May: May 4-8 and May 11-15.
Make-up testing is also available during the third week of May. Therefore, the administration is recommending changing the compensatory day from May 8, 2026, to June 4, 2026. This would mean the teachers' work day would be June 3, 2026, and the students' last day would be June 2,
2026.
Our current 2025-2026 school year calendar has a professional development day on April 24,
2026. The district administration is recommending changing the professional development day to
May 1, 2026. As the district thinks about staffing changes that may be the result of potential school closures, it is in the best interest of staff and school culture to have a professional development date set aside for staff to come together with their new school communities prior to the start of the 2026-27. By pushing the professional development day out one week, this would allow for another week of staffing to take place and have a larger number of staff settled into their roles for the 2026-27 school year.
