Minutes of Business Meeting and Public Hearing
The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools
**Approved January 5, 2026
A Business Meeting of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools (RAS) and Public Hearing was held Monday,
December 15, 2025, beginning at 6:00 p.m. in the Boardroom at the Robbinsdale Area Schools Education Service
Center (ESC). A recording of the meeting can be found at: https://www.rdale.org/discover/school-board under "Watch
School Board Meeting Webcasts."
Welcome and Land Acknowledgement
Call to Order/Roll Call
Directors present: Bassett, Bowman, Evans-Becker, Hillenbrand, Long, and Wutoh; and Dr. Teri Staloch,
Superintendent. Absent: none. There was a quorum, and the meeting was called to order.
Acceptance of the Agenda
MOTION: Wutoh moved approval of the agenda, Bowman seconded.
Motion prevailed with a 6-0 voice vote.
Consent Agenda
Consent Agenda items include administrative matters, personnel reports, and financial reports.
MOTION: Bassett moved to remove Administrative Item 2 - 2026-2027 District Calendar for further discussion and approve the remainder of the Consent Agenda items, Hillenbrand seconded.
Motion prevailed with a 6-0 roll call vote.
MOTION: Bassett moved approval of the 2026-2027 District Calendar, Bowman seconded.
Voting in favor: Bowman, Evans-Becker
Voting against: Bassett, Hillenbrand, Long, Wutoh
Motion failed with a 2-4 roll call vote. Calendar to be discussed at a future Study Session.
Unfinished Business
A. Resolution for 2026 Election Combined Polling Locations
MOTION: Bowman moved approval of the polling locations, Bassett seconded.
Motion prevailed with a 6-0 roll call vote.
New Business
A. Resolution for Acceptance of Donations in the amount of $9,213.91
MOTION: Hillenbrand moved approval of the donations, Bassett seconded.
Motion prevailed with a 6-0 roll call vote.
B. Proposed Change to 2025-26 School Board Meeting Calendar for addition Listening Time on January 5, 2026
MOTION: Bowman moved approval of the proposed calendar change, Wutoh seconded.
Motion prevailed with a 6-0 voice vote.
C. Proposed Change to 2025-26 School Board Meeting Calendar for additional Listening Time on December 22,
2025
MOTION: Bowman moved approval of the proposed calendar change, Bassett seconded.
Motion prevailed with a 6-0 voice vote.
Listening Time will be the only item on the agenda December 22, and will continue until all speakers have been heard.
Policy
Second Read (Action) - Policies
MOTION: Wutoh moved approval of the changes to the following policies, Bowman seconded:
● 401 Policy - Equal Employment Opportunity
● 403 Policy - Discipline, Suspension and Dismissal of School District Employees
● 404 Policy - Employment Background Checks
● 406 Policy - Public and Private Personnel Data
● 408 - Subpoena of a School District Employee
Motion prevailed with a 6-0 voice vote.
Public Hearing

A. Necessity and Practicability Statement - Dr. Bob McDowell, Assistant Superintendent
B. Public Testimony
C. Board Deliberation
D. Board Action by Resolution a. Resolution Closing Robbinsdale Middle School (RMS)
MOTION: Chair Evans-Becker moved approval of the resolution and waived reading, Wutoh seconded.
Voting in favor: Bassett, Bowman, Evans-Becker, Hillenbrand, Wutoh
Voting against: Long
Motion prevailed with a 5-1 roll call vote. RMS will close no later than the start of the 2026-2027 school year.
b. Resolution Closing Lakeview Elementary School (LVE)
MOTION: Bowman moved approval of the resolution and waived reading, Hillenbrand seconded.
Voting in favor: Hillenbrand
Voting against: Bassett, Bowman, Evans-Becker, Long, Wutoh
Motion failed with a 1-5 roll call vote. LVE will remain open.
c. Resolution Closing Neill Elementary School (ENE)
MOTION: Bowman moved approval of the resolution and waived reading, Wutoh seconded.
Voting in favor: None
Voting against: Bowman, Evans-Becker, Hillenbrand, Long, Wutoh
Abstaining: Bassett
Motion failed with 5 votes against in a roll call vote. ENE will remain open.
d. Resolution Closing Noble Elementary School (NOE)
MOTION: Bowman moved approval of the resolution and waived reading, Hillenbrand seconded.
Voting in favor: Bowman, Evans-Becker, Hillenbrand, Wutoh
Voting against: Bassett, Long
Motion prevailed with a 4-2 roll call vote. NOE will close no later than the start of the 2026-2027 school year.
e. Resolution Closing Sonnesyn Elementary School (SOE)
MOTION: Bowman moved approval of the resolution and waived reading, Bassett seconded.
Voting in favor: Bowman, Evans-Becker, Hillenbrand, Wutoh
Voting against: Bassett, Long
Motion prevailed with a 4-2 roll call vote. SOE will close no later than the start of the 2026-2027 school year.
f. Resolution Closint the Education Service Center (ESC)
MOTION: Bowman moved approval of the resolution and waived reading, Hillenbrand seconded.
Voting in favor: Bowman, Evans-Becker, Hillenbrand, Wutoh
Voting against: Bassett, Long
Motion prevailed with a 4-2 roll call vote. ESC will close.
Future Events (can be found on our website)
Adjournment
MOTION: Bowman moved to adjourn the meeting, and Wutoh seconded the motion.
Motion prevailed with a 6-0 voice vote. Meeting was adjourned at 9:37 p.m.
