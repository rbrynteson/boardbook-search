Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent

RE: 36th Avenue N. Cost Sharing Agreement with City of Plymouth -
ACTION
th
The School Board is asked to approve the 36 Avenue N. Cost Sharing Agreement with the City of Plymouth.
th
The agreement calls for a cost sharing agreement for the construction work on 36 Avenue, th for improvement to 36 Avenue N. and Plymouth Middle School. The agreement will improve access to the traffic flow, reduce congestion and improve public safety. The project will be coordinated by the City of Plymouth to include solicitation of bids, bid awards, and granting easements. The City of Plymouth will invoice the school district for their portion of the cost when the project is complete.
The District will grant the City permanent easements along the entire northern border of the middle school to facilitate the project as shown on Exhibit B. The District will also grant a temporary construction easement as shown on the attached Exhibit B. The City will pay the district just compensation for these easements, as mutually agreed upon by the parties.
Each party will be responsible for its share of the total project cost.
