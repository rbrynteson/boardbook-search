To: School Board and Superintendent Staloch
From: Kristen Hoheisel, Chief Financial Officer
Date: August 4, 2025
RE: Nutrition Services Fund Transfer
As a part of the Minnesota Department of Education (MDE) resource management review, MDE
provided guidance to Nutrition Services around purchasing card rebates. Specifically, Nutrition
Services used Nonprofit School Food Service Account (NSFSA) funds to pay for some food service expenses using a purchasing card (p-card). As the p-card generates rebates that are credited to the General Fund, in accordance with 2 CFR 200.406, all allowable food services expenses must be net of applicable credits, including rebates.
Based on FY25 Nutrition Services p-card expenditures, $277.39 for p-card rebates must be allocated to the NSFSA (fund 02). Per guidance from MDE, this rebate amount must be restored via a fund transfer in the current (review) fiscal year (FY25) and allocated to Source Code 649.
A journal entry reflecting this is attached.
