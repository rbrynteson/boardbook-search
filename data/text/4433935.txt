507 POLICY - CORPORAL PUNISHMENT AND PRONE RESTRAINT
I. PURPOSE
The purpose of this policy is to describe limitations on use of corporal punishment and prone restraint upon a student.
II. GENERAL STATEMENT OF POLICY
No employee or agent of the school district shall inflict corporal punishment or use prone restraint upon a student.
III. PROCESS
The superintendent is directed to develop administrative procedures for Corporal Punishment and Prone Restraint for the School District that meet the state and federal requirements established in law or rule.
