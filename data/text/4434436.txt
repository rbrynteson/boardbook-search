ROBBINSDALE

Area Schools Minutes of Special Work Session
The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools

**Reviewed XXXX

A Special Work Session of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools (RAS) was held Tuesday, November 14, 2023.
Complete agendas, reports, and presentations are available at the office and on our website. Work session summary minutes are not approved by the School Board.

The Special Work Session was called to order at 5:03 p.m.

Director(s) present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Sharon E. Brooks, Greta Evans-Becker, Kim Holmes, Caroline Long and John Vento; and Marti Voight, Interim Superintendent. Director(s) absent: none.

Welcome
Helen Bassett, School Board Chair

Review/Selection of Superintendent Search Firms to Present to the School Board on Monday, November 20, 2023
at the Work Session

The School Board directors each provided their top three superintendent search firm selections from a pool of six.

Upon hearing from each director, discussion in regards to which three rose to the top of the list were determined, with an agreement by all to interview four in total. The four firms advancing will interview on Monday, November 20, 2023 during the work session, after which the School Board will determine their final selection in an effort to have a contract in place by the end of December. The School Board will officially begin their superintendent search in January of 2024 with the selected firm.

Adjournment
The Special Work Session adjourned at 5:47 p.m.
