Administrative Reports
A. Superintendent's Report - Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent
Superintendent Staloch talked about the district's mission to inspire and educate all learners to develop their unique potential and positively contribute to their community, and how RAS is committed to ensuring every student graduates career, articulated trades and college ready. Highlights of the presentation included:
● Welcoming students and staff back to school
● Creating better learning environments with building projects
● New Forest Elementary playground created in partnership with the City of Crystal
● Summer of Transition: three buildings closed, Highview/RVA moved to FAIR Crystal - FAIR Crystal programming moved to PMS and SMS
● Building Better Together : 2026 Referendum Update
