Summary Minutes of Study (Work) Session
ROBBINSDALE

Area Schools The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools

*"*Reviewed February 3, 2025

A Study (Work) Session of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools (RAS) was held Monday, January 21, 2025 at
9:33 p.m. in the Boardroom at the Robbinsdale Area Schools Education Service Center (ESC). Complete agendas, reports, and presentations are available at the office and on our website. A recording of the meeting can be found at:
https://www.rdale.org/discover/school-board under "Watch School Board Meeting Webcasts." Study (Work) Session summary minutes are not approved by the School Board.

Director(s) present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Dr. Greta Evans-Becker, Aviva Hillenbrand, Kim Holmes, and
Caroline Long; and Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent. Director(s) absent: none.

Introductions
All in attendance introduced themselves.

Superintendent Mid-Year Evaluation Presentation

Dr. Teri Staloch

Dr. Staloch provided a presentation of her accomplishments, and reviewed the timeline. Dr. Staloch's presentation is posted in the agenda on the website for review. Discussion and questions followed. After the Study (Work) Session concluded, the Board entered a closed session to work collaboratively on feedback for Dr. Staloch, per her contract.

Board Topic: Board Response to Editorial Letter

Dr. Greta Evans-Becker, School Board Chair

Mick Waldspurger, Attorney from Squires, Waldspurger and Mace

The Board reviewed a resolution provided by Mr. Waldspurger in response to the editorial published in the Sun Post by
Director Brooks. Discussion and questions followed. The resolution/statement responding will be brought for action at the
Business Meeting on February 3, 2025.

Work Session adjourned at 10:40 p.m.
