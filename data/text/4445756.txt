To: School Board and Superintendent Staloch
Date: June 16, 2025
RE: Designation of Official Television Producer for Events Conducted by the MSHSL for 2025-26
Robbinsdale Area Schools, ISD 281, designates Northwest Community Television - CCX Media, of
Brooklyn Park as its 2025-26 educational television producer for events conducted by the
Minnesota High School League when the district is unable to cover the events.
It is also understood that this designation gives clearance to Northwest Community Television -
CCX Media to cover regional and sectional events in which students from Robbinsdale Area School may be involved.
