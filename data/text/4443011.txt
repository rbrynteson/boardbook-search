Minutes of Work Session
The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools
**Reviewed March 6, 2023
A Work Session of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools was held Tuesday, February 21, 2023. Complete agendas, reports, and presentations are available at the office and on our website. Work session summary minutes are not approved by the School Board.
Director(s) present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Sharon Brooks Green, Greta Evans-Becker, Kim Holmes, Caroline
Long, and John Vento; and David Engstrom, Superintendent. Director(s) absent: none.
Policy 206
The School Board had a discussion regarding MSBA Policy 206: Public Participation in School Board
Meetings/Complaints about Persons at School Board Meetings and Data Privacy Considerations, which is coming for consideration by the Policy Governance Committee. This discussion was to provide guidance to the Policy Governance
Committee about listening time/public comment as they prepare to review this policy.
