To: Robbinsdale Area Schools Board of Education
Re: 2024 Student Opinion Survey Results
Robbinsdale Area Schools conducts an annual student opinion survey with students in grades four through 12. The purpose of the survey is to gain student perspectives on issues related to their sense of belonging in school and their impressions of teacher expectations, diversity, school safety, and fair discipline. The survey items will be reviewed during the 2024-25 school year with the next administration planned for spring of 2026. In the spring of 2025, district students will participate in the Minnesota Student Survey rather than the district survey.
The survey is completed online and is confidential. The survey includes 32-42 questions and students are asked to rate various topics as strongly agree, agree, disagree, and strongly disagree.
Results from this year's survey are below.
Sense of Belonging
84% of students report a sense of belonging at school. At the elementary and middle school level,
84-86% of students agreed or strongly agreed with the statement "I feel like I belong at this school" since at least 2017. At the high school level, 82-86% agreed with the statement each time the survey has been administered since 2007.
91% of elementary students, 78% of middle school students, and 77% of high school students believe their teacher(s) care about them. These percentages have been consistent over time. Since
2015, 90-92% of elementary students, 76-80% of middle school students, and 73-78% of high school students indicated that their teachers care about them.
9 out of 10 students feel there is at least one adult at school that cares about them. This has been fairly consistent across several survey administrations.
90% of elementary, 86% of middle school, and 93% of high school students report teachers treat them with respect.
18% of students in grades 4-8 and 22% of high school students indicate they are often lonely at school. 83% of elementary students and more than 93% of secondary students tell us they have at least one friend at school.
Teaching and Learning
Students consistently report that teachers in Robbinsdale Area Schools hold high expectations of them. 95%-99% of students agree "My teacher expects me to do my best in class."

Most students are comfortable asking their teachers for help - 83% of elementary students, 77% of middle school students, and 79% of high school students. At the elementary, this is down slightly from before the pandemic (89% in 2020).
80% of middle school students and 77% of high school students report they are learning a lot from their classes. At the elementary level, 78% of students report that their ideas are valued by adults.
This is down slightly from before the pandemic (82% in 2020). Four out of five elementary students report that they have opportunities to be a leader in their classroom. This is also down slightly from 86% in 2020.
Sense of Community
Four out of five (81%) elementary students indicate that adults at their school deal appropriately with racist behavior (the same as in 2019). This is down from a high of 86% agreement in 2020. In middle school, 62% of students agreed with this statement, down from the 65-66% trend since
2017. At the high school level, more students are agreeing that adults are dealing appropriately with racist behavior (68%) than any other year since 2017, up from 61% agreement in 2020.
Secondary students were asked, "During a typical week how often do students who misbehave keep you from doing your work?" Across time, 14-19% of middle school students have this issue frequently (more than five times in a typical week). The exception was in 2023 when only 8% of middle school students had this issue frequently. In 2024, the percentage of high school students reporting that the misbehavior of other students frequently kept them from doing their work was at its lowest point since 2015 (5% compared to 8-10% in other years).
Secondary students were also asked, "During a typical week how often are you harassed or picked on by other students at this school?" In 2024, 7% of middle school students responded that this happened frequently. On previous administrations of the survey, 3-6% of middle school students indicated this was an issue more than five times a week. In high school, 2% of students report that they are harassed or picked on frequently, down from 5% in 2023.
Sense of Safety
Just over 90% of high school students report feeling safe in the classroom. For middle school students, 88-90% of students have reported feeling safe in the classroom since 2017. At the elementary level, the percentage has decreased slightly in the last two administrations from 91%
in 2020 to 87% in 2024. In addition, 8 out of 10 students report feeling safe in the hallway. Three out of five high school students, 70% of middle school students, and 75% of elementary students report feeling safe in the bathroom. Higher percentages of students report feeling safe in the cafeteria - 86% at the secondary level and 87% at the elementary level.
