Resource Officers (SRO's) in 281 schools. When SRO's are in place, their services utilize 90%
of the Safe School Levy.

Ad Hoc Committee's Recommendation for Sandy Hook Promise Programs:
The Ad Hoc Committee recommends the Board of Education recommit to the Sandy
Hook Promise Programs with a directive to Administration to better utilize all SHP has to offer in terms of educational components, training, marketing documents, etc.
Recommended directives include:
1. Schedule a Board of Education meeting with Sandy Hook Promise representatives
(virtual session would suffice)
2. Produce a detailed marketing plan to reach all stakeholders regarding the SHP
programs, including the SHP Anonymous Reporting App, being available to community members, business owners and families
3. Administration guarantee to ensure all District devices having the ability to use the
SHP Anonymous Reporting App no later than 2/16/2024.
4. Connect the student groups who participated in the Youth Summit to the students who participated in the SHP training
5. Produce an elementary schools plan on how Start with Hello is being started
6. BOE be provided a monthly report from the students SAVE (Students Against
Violence Everywhere) groups
7. Monthly - BOE be given the report from the SHP Anonymous Reporting App
