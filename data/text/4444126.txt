To: Board of Education
Date: April 2, 2024
Re: Requested information regarding Capital Projects Levy
In 2014 Robbinsdale Area Schools (Rdale) received a voter-approved Capital Projects Levy - a term used in state law - to fund specific technology and safety projects. This kind of levy helps fund an approved capital project, at a term of 10 years. Last year, Rdale collected $5.9 million on the capital projects levy.
Rdale's current Capital Project Levy authorizes the district to fund:
● Annual technology purchases
○ Replacement network hardware
○ Annual software subscriptions
● Recurring technology purchases
○ Devices for students (Chromebooks and iPads), teachers and non-licensed staff
○ Network infrastructure
○ Classroom technology
● Long-term technology projects
○ ReplacementSMART boards
Supporting documents: Replacement cycle | How the levy dollars have been spent
Rdale's proposed Capital Project Levy:
The existing percentage paid by taxpayers will remain at its current rate of 4.207%, which will provide the district approximately $6.64 million in FY 2024-25 and beyond, which will be used in two ways:
○ To maintain current technology investments, the district will need $4.64 million annually in capital levy funds.
○ The remaining $2 million in funds would be used to improve safety and security at our schools by providing capital improvements, including security cameras and safety-related building modifications, such as secure entrances.
○ Funding the district receives from the Capital Projects Levy must only be used on projects and equipment that are detailed in the capital levy plan. Dollars cannot be redirected to the general fund or used elsewhere.
Recommendation:
Put the Capital Projects Levy renewal on the 2024 ballot this November. The levy would continue to fund technology, and the scope would be increased to add safety and security.
It's important that the election be held this coming fall. In the event voters approve the levy renewal November of 2024, the property taxes would be collected starting in 2025 for the 2025-26
school year. If the levy election were to be delayed until 2025, assuming it's approved, the funds wouldn't be available until the 2026-27 school year, meaning we'd have a funding gap the 2025-26
school year of $6.64 million.
