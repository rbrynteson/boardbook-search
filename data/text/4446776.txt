New Business
AGENDA SECTION:
8.B. Adjustment to 2025-26 District Calendar
ITEM:
Dr. Bob McDowell, Assistant Superintendent
COMMENTS BY:
District administration is recommending a calendar change allowing for June 1, 2026 to be the last student contact day for grades K-8, and June 2, 2026 to be a non-student contact day for grades K-8 to allow for necessary packing for teachers impacted by school closures and consolidation.
Recommended Action: Approve the 2025-26 proposed calendar changes.
