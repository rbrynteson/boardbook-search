What happens if the referendum doesn't pass?
● Rdale remains in statutory operating debt (SOD)
● An additional $2-3 million in reductions needed for
2027-28, regardless of the referendum outcome
● Without referendum funding, the district would still need to reduce its facility footprint
● Reductions could extend further into student programming, staffing and services
● All four operating areas would be considered: facilities, transportation, programming and staffing

What reductions could be considered?
● Schools and grade configurations: High school consolidation, grade-level restructuring and additional boundary changes
● Educational programming: Potential reductions to art, music, Advanced Placement and Career and
Technical Education
● Athletics and activities: Fewer sports and extracurricular opportunities
● Class sizes and staffing: Larger classes, fewer dedicated classrooms and teachers moving between rooms
● Transportation: Increased walking distances for bus eligibility

What happens next?
● No reductions have been approved: All potential options require further evaluation and Board consideration
● Oct. 19 Board study session: Continued discussion of
2027-28 budget reductions
● Facility needs remain: Aging buildings and operational challenges must still be addressed
● Cost of waiting: Delaying improvements could increase future construction costs
● Our commitment: Transparent communication about decisions and potential impacts on students
