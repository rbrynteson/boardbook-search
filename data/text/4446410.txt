Special Education Programming in Robbinsdale Area Schools
Federal setting refers to the amount of time a student receives special education services away from their non-disabled peers.
● Setting I: 0-20% of time spent in special education.
● Setting II: 21-60% of time spent in special education.
● Setting III: 61-100% of time spent in special education.
● Setting IV: Special education services in a separate school for more than 50% of the school day.

Special Education Programming in Robbinsdale Area Schools
Setting I and II special education services are provided in all
K-12 Rdale Schools.
Setting III (center-based) special education services are provided at selected sites. The number of classrooms and their location are modified annually based on the unique student needs.
Setting IV special education services are provided by
Intermediate District 287.
