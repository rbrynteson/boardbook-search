Armstrong High School senior Lucy Dreyer has earned the National Speech and
Debate Association's (NSDA) Academic All-American Award.
The NSDA Academic All-American Award is presented to 500 students annually who show academic excellence, leadership, service and integrity in the Debate and Speech community. Lucy is the twelfth Armstrong Student to achieve this recognition, and this is the seventh year in a row an Armstrong student has achieved such an honor.
Over the past three years, Lucy has been honored numerous times - including NWSC
All-Conference and a national qualifier in Debate in 2023.
The NSDA is the largest interscholastic organization in the world recognizing students through speech and debate competition, as well as community service, public speaking and leadership activities. Founded in 1925, the NSDA has celebrated the achievements of more than 1.5 million alumni.
We are proud of Miss Dreyer and her achievements!
