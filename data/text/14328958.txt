To: Robbinsdale Area School Board
From: Kristen Hoheisel, Chief Financial Officer
Date: August 25, 2026
Re: Resolution to close the OPEB Debt Service Fund
Overview:
The OPEB Debt Service Fund was established when long-term debt was issued for the specific purpose of funding a trust account to help cover other post employment debt (OPEB).
The bonds that were issued reached full maturity and the final principal and interest payments are completely satisfied.
Therefore, based on accounting guidelines and auditor advice, administration recommends closing out our OPEB Debt Service account and transferring any residual balances to our regular
Debt Service Fund. This will eliminate an inactive account from the district's annual reporting.
Upon school board action, administration will make the appropriate entries to completely close out the OPEB Debt Service Fund effective at the end of the 2025-2026 fiscal year.
