To: Members of the School Board
Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent
From: Kristen Hoheisel, Chief Financial Officer
Date: September 16, 2024
Re: Contract with School Management Services, LLC
RECOMMENDATION: Administration recommends School Board approval of the Business
Management Support Services contract from School Management Services, LLC
DISCUSSION: I am requesting approval to enter into an agreement for business management services of Todd Netzke of School Management Services to include:
● Budget Development Support & Monitoring
● Monthly Board Financials Preparation and Presentation
● Long Range Financial Projections Development (Budget File & Frontline)
● General Accounting Support
● Other Special Projects upon request
The fee for providing business management support will be $150 per hour. The contract will be executed as needed. This arrangement will remain in effect until notice is given by either party to terminate the arrangement.
The District has been under contract with School Management Services since December 1,
2023 and was approved at the December 18, 2023 School Board Meeting. The existing contract had a 30 day termination clause. The new contract will take effect on September 1, 2024 and will replace that contract
