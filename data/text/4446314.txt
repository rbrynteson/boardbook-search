6.B. Annual Support of Form B Grant Application to the
ITEM:
Minnesota State High School League (MSHSL) Foundation
Anthony Williams, Executive Director of Community
COMMENTS BY:
Education, Athletics and Activities
Mr. Williams requests approval of a resolution to support the Form B Grant Application to the Minnesota State High School League (MSHSL) Foundation. The purpose of this grant is to provide funding to assist school districts in three specific areas including leadership initiatives, safety, and transportation that reduces barriers to participation.
The MSHSL Foundation is a nonprofit association that promotes the growth of high school students through participation in valuable extra-curricular activities.
The MSHSL Foundation is a nonprofit association. The Foundation awards grants to assist, recognize, promote, and fund extra-curricular participation by high school students in athletic and fine arts programs. Grants are awarded based upon gender balance, school size, and geographic location to the extent possible.
Recommended Action: Approve the resolutions to support the Form B Grant
Application to the MSHSL Foundation.
