AGENDA SECTION:
5.C. Resolution Form B1 Grant - MSHSL Foundation
ITEM:
Anthony Williams, Executive Director of Community
COMMENTS BY:
Education, Athletics and Activities
The Superintendent will request approval to apply for the Form B1 Grant to the Minnesota
State High School League (MSHSL) Foundation. The purpose of this grant is to school districts with transportation costs associated with reducing barriers and providing the opportunity for students to attend the Together We Make A Difference World Cafés held across the state. Robbinsdale Armstrong and Cooper High School students and staff attended the day-long session at Park Center Senior High School in Brooklyn Park, on
January 13, 2023. The estimated transportation cost is $280. Sixteen high school students attended the World Café.
The World Café conversation is part of a statewide initiative to ensure students are provided an atmosphere to create supportive school environments for activities and athletics that are free from racial, gender, and religious harm.
Recommended motion: Approve the resolution Form B1 Grant - MSHSL Foundation .
