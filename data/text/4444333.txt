AVID's Mission
To close the opportunity gap by preparing all students for college readiness and success in a global society.
3

What is AVID?
AVID stands for Advancement Via Individual
Determination and is a college readiness system designed to help students develop the skills they need to be successful in college.
4

AVID's Purpose
AVID levels the playing field for students without a college-going tradition in their families and/or students who are underrepresented on four-year college campuses.
AVID is designed to:
● increase overall academic performance
● success in rigorous curriculum
● participation in activities at school
AVID helps students become better educated, responsible citizens, and leaders in society.
5

RAS AVID History
2005-2006 - 1st year of AVID in the high schools
2009-2010 - 1st year of AVID in the middle schools
Initial exploration and funding provided by a grant through Cargill and became a part of our Achievement and Integration plan

Schoolwide AVID & AVID Elective
There are 2 components to AVID:
➔ Schoolwide AVID - all students are exposed to and learn AVID strategies from staff throughout the building. Building provides professional learning and strategy sharing. Potential building wide goals: planner usage, Focused Note-taking, etc.
➔ AVID Elective - students apply and enter the AVID
Elective and receive specific curriculum and instruction with the focus on being college and career ready.

AVID Schoolwide

AVID Elective
In AVID, students learn organizational and study skills, work on critical thinking and asking probing questions, get academic help from peers and tutors, and participate in enrichment and motivational activities that make them college and career-ready.
Two days a week - AVID Curriculum
Two days a week - Tutorials
One day a week - "Framework Friday" including community building activities, guest speakers, college visits
