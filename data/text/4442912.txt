Agenda Item 5: Quick Discussion
Two questions were presented for FAC feedback:
• What are ways that Robbinsdale can build more trust in the community with school finances?
• In doing so, how can Robbinsdale be more equitably impactful in how we allocate resources?
A brief discussion occurred focusing on the importance of transparency of the District's financial information and actions, and the idea of reaching out to (and inviting in for discussion) diverse district stakeholders.
Agenda Item 6: FAC Budget Timeline
Mr. Dozier presented the fiscal year 2023 FAC meeting schedule including a list of topics for each meeting.
Agenda Item 7: Proposed Levy Payable 2023 Update
Mr. Dozier presented the District's FY23 levy amounts, with a line-by-line comparison to FY18 through
FY22. He discussed the reasons for some of the larger changes. On December 19, 2022, the District is to adopt the final levy at the ISD 281 scheduled school board meeting. December 28, 2022 is the deadline to submit the final levy to Hennepin County and January 7, 2023 is the deadline to submit the final levy to the Minnesota Department of Education.
Mr. Vento clarified that the state legislature sets the maximum levy, and the Board makes the final decision regarding the levy amount. The preliminary levy for Pay 23 was set at the maximum dollar amount allowed (as has been the case in prior years).
Agenda Item 8: Enrollment Update - Enrollment Variance and ADM Comparison
Mr. Dozier presented the District's enrollment figures as of October 14, 2022. The District's budgeted average daily membership (ADM) for fiscal year 2023 is 10,763 which is 188 less than the actual 10,909
October 14, 2023 seat count. Mr. Dozier noted that the District has taken a more conservative approach in budgeting ADM. The positive variance of actual versus budgeted students was heavily driven by students in grades 4, 9, and 12. The revised budget will reflect the increase in revenue.
