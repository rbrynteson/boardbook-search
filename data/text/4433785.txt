Applitrack Update
As we transition to the new fiscal year we will be posting positions in Applitrack differently. Each position will have its own job number, and there will no longer be bulk postings per category. This will help us monitor individual postings versus department postings. On July 1, we will zero out all of the positions for the 2022-2023 school year and track the open positions for the 2023-2024 school year.
