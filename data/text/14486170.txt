RECOMMENDATION:
It is recommended that the School Board approve the renewal rates for our self-insured health and dental insurance programs for the 2027 plan year. The District's Labor
Management Committee met on September 14, 2026, voted, and approved the rates summarized below.
MEDICAL RENEWAL DISCUSSION:
The district's self-funded health insurance renewal for 2027 is part of the HITA marketing.
The district received proposals from HealthEZ (incumbent), BCBS of MN and PEIP.
All proposals were presented to the LMC with voting and approval to accept the proposal from HealthEZ. The LMC approved a funding increase of 6.1%. There will be one IRS
required plan change to the $1700 HSA compatible plan moving the single deductible to
$1750. All other plan designs and programs will stay the same. The funding rates for 2027
are as follows:
