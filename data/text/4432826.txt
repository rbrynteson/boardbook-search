5.D. Resolution Form B2 Grant - MSHSL Foundation
ITEM:
Anthony Williams, Executive Director of Community
COMMENTS BY:
Education, Athletics and Activities
The Superintendent will request approval to apply for the Form B2 Grant to the Minnesota
State High School League (MSHSL) Foundation. The purpose of this grant is to provide funding to assist school districts in three specific areas including leadership initiatives, safety, and transportation that reduces barriers to participation. The MSHSL Foundation is a nonprofit association that promotes the growth of high school students through participation in valuable extra-curricular activities.
Recommended motion: Approve the resolution Form B2 Grant - MSHSL Foundation .
