ROBBINSDALE

Area Schools Minutes of Closed Session
The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools

*"Approved June 4, 2024

A Closed Session of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools, ISD 281, was held Monday,
May 20, 2024, beginning at 5:00 p.m. in the Superintendent's Office at the Robbinsdale Area Schools
Education Service Center, 4148 Winnetka Avenue North, New Hope, Minnesota.

1. Call to Order and Roll Call
Chair ReNae Bowman called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.

Directors present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Sharon E. Brooks, Dr. Greta Evans-Becker,
Kim Holmes, and Caroline Long

Directors absent: John Vento

Other attendees:
e Marti Voight, Interim Superintendent e John Groenke, Executive Director of Student Services e Bo Powell, Director of Security

2. School Board
MOTION: Chair Bowman moved to move to a Closed session pursuant to Minnesota
Statutes 13D.05 subdivisions 2 (a)(3) and subdivision 3(d) to receive security briefings, and discuss all facilities' security systems and emergency response procedures - Director
Evans-Becker seconded the motion. Upon vote being taken thereon, the following voted in favor thereof: Directors Bassett, Bowman, Brooks, Evans-Becker, Holmes, and Long. And the following voted against the same; none. Said motion was declared duly passed. Recessed to closed session at 5:01 p.m.

Returned from closed session at 5:25 p.m.

3. Adjournment
MOTION: Director Evans-Becker moved adjournment, Director Brooks seconded the motion.
Upon vote being taken thereon, the following voted in favor thereof: Directors Bassett, Bowman,
Brooks, Evans-Becker, Holmes, and Long. And the following voted against the same; none. Said motion was declared duly passed. Meeting adjourned at 5:26 p.m.
