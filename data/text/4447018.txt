Arts & Innovation
As recommended by the ReImagine 2030 Vision team
● Part of rebranding the district identity
● Arts and Innovation programming across all schools, including Spanish Immersion.
● Facilities intentionally redesigned and renovated, where necessary, to include functional learning spaces
(e.g., maker spaces, outdoor labs, performance arts spaces).
● Building on partnerships, like those that already exist with the Minnesota Dept. of Natural Resources (SEA)
and Stages Theatre Company (FAIR), would be expanded across the district.
How we get there
● Engage staff to incorporate the most effective initiatives from our schools
● District-wide focus on hands-on learning, critical thinking, problem-solving, creativity, and real-world readiness-skills that are often built through project-based learning.
● Blur lines between traditional disciplines through integration of arts, technology, and processes
● Intentional design spaces and experiences for each level in our system
