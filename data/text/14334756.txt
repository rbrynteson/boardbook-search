A. Superintendent's Report - Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent
Superintendent Staloch talked about the district's mission to inspire and educate all learners to develop their unique potential and positively contribute to their community, and how RAS is committed to ensuring every student graduates career, articulated trades and college ready. Highlights of the presentation included:
● Rdale leaders preparing for the year ahead
● Staff returning with new teachers first on August 18, then starting August 24 with workshop week
● Information for the school year on our website, and in the Rdale Roundup mailer
● First day of school is Monday, August 31, 2026

● Building Better Together: Connecting with Communities bond referendum presentations to cities and community
