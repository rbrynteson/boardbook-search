RESOLUTION TO CLOSE THE OPEB DEBT SERVICE FUND AND TRANSFER REMAINING BALANCE
Independent School District No. 281
Robbinsdale Area Schools
Hennepin County, Minnesota
WHEREAS, Independent School District No. 281 (the "District") established an Other
Post-Employment Benefits ("OPEB") Debt Service Fund for the purpose of accounting for resources and expenditures related to the District's OPEB debt obligations; and
WHEREAS, the District has completed the obligations and financial activities for which the OPEB
Debt Service Fund was established; and
WHEREAS, the District has determined that the OPEB Debt Service Fund is no longer necessary as a separate fund for the ongoing accounting of OPEB debt service activity; and
WHEREAS, upon completion of the District's financial activity for the 2025-2026 fiscal year, the
OPEB Debt Service Fund may have a remaining fund balance; and
WHEREAS, the District desires to close the OPEB Debt Service Fund at the conclusion of the
2025-2026 fiscal year and transfer any remaining balance to the District's Debt Service Fund, subject to applicable Minnesota statutes, Minnesota Department of Education requirements, and generally accepted accounting principles applicable to Minnesota school districts;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the School Board of Independent School District No. 281,
Robbinsdale Area Schools, as follows:
1. Closure of OPEB Debt Service Fund. The OPEB Debt Service Fund shall be closed effective June 30, 2026, upon completion of all required year-end accounting and closing procedures.
2. Transfer of Remaining Balance. Any remaining fund balance or other available resources in the OPEB Debt Service Fund as of June 30, 2026, after payment or provision for all outstanding obligations and required closing entries, are hereby authorized to be transferred to the District's Debt Service Fund.
3. Accounting and Financial Reporting. The District administration is authorized and directed to make all accounting entries necessary to effectuate the closure of the OPEB

Debt Service Fund and the transfer authorized by this resolution and to appropriately report the transaction in the District's financial records and annual financial statements.
4. Administrative Authority. The Superintendent and Chief Financial Officer, or their designees, are authorized to take all actions necessary to implement this resolution, including coordination with the District's auditors and the Minnesota Department of
Education, as appropriate.
5. Effective Date. This resolution shall be effective upon its adoption by the School Board.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the School Board hereby authorizes the closure of the OPEB Debt
Service Fund and the transfer of any remaining balance to the District's Debt Service Fund as described herein.
