Mission
The mission of Robbinsdale Area
Schools is to inspire and educate all learners to develop their unique potential and positively contribute to their community.

Preparing for 2026-27
Although the students are no longer in session, over the past week, staff gathered to continue learning and improving.
System Leaders Advance:
● All of the district administrators from departments and schools met for the day on Tuesday, June 9, to review and reflect on the 2025-26 school year and continue to reimagine improvement in all areas.
School Improvement Planning:
● The Teaching and Learning Team and Instructional Leadership brought together over 125 teachers and administrators to collaborate on a new and improved focus of the School Improvement Plans.

Rdale Earns State Recognition
Robbinsdale Area Schools received Bronze
Berry honors from the Minnesota Department of Agriculture.
● Recognized for serving
Minnesota-grown foods throughout the school year
● Local produce, dairy, grains, and culturally relevant menu items featured in meals
● Partnerships with Minnesota farmers, producers, and food businesses support student nutrition
● Farm-to-School efforts connect students to food, culture, and community

Rdale Educator Earns State Honor
Parent and Family Educator Michelle
Berscheid has received the 2026 Lois
Engstrom Leadership Award.
● Recognized by MNAFEE for outstanding leadership in parent education and early childhood programming
● Career dedicated to strengthening families through education, literacy, and community connection
● State and national leader in family education and parent engagement
● Honors the impact of supporting families during the earliest years of a child's development

Summer Meals Available for Students
Free summer meals are available to children and teens across
Robbinsdale Area Schools.
● Meals available at multiple sites throughout the summer
● Free for all children ages 1-18, regardless of enrollment status
● Helps ensure students have access to nutritious meals when school is out
● Families can find locations, dates, and meal times at rdale.org
