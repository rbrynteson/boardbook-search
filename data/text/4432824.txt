Administrative Reports
A. Superintendent's Report
Superintendent Staloch talked about the district's mission to inspire and educate all learners to develop their unique potential and positively contribute to their community, and how RAS is committed to ensuring every student graduates career, articulated trades and college ready. Highlights of the presentation included:
•
Eleven Armstrong and seven Cooper students completed a semester-long course culminating in passing
EMR and CPR certifications
•
Since 2019 RTCoffee has served as a way for Robbinsdale Transition Center (RTC) students in the Money for Independent Living class to experience a faux economy, working and earning paychecks, and learning how to earn and budget money
•
Robbinsdale has had 12 students selected for the American Choral Directors Association of Minnesota
(ACDA-MN) honor choirs
•
Board directors have attended mid-year school visits across the district with Superintendent Staloch
•
Bridget Dooley - Northport Interim Principal, Jessica Schultz - MTSS Instructional Coach, and Laura
Suckerman - K-5 Literacy Specialist were joined by Kristin Burger - Director of Literacy at ServeMN, to present the MESPA Institute 2026 Conference regarding Bridge2Read
•
Board directors, legislatiors, Senior Leadership Team members and students recently participated in Dignity
Index sessions
•
All Invited to the Black History Celebration at Robbinsdale Middle School on Friday, Feb. 20 from 5:30-7:30
p.m.
