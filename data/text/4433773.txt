TIMELINE FOR 2023-24
EC-12 EDUCATION ASSISTANT STAFFING
Spring 2023
April 25 Deadline for principals and program directors to update EA staffing documents.
April 26-28 HR staffing meeting with RFT representatives.
May 5 Initial EA openings, displacements, layoffs and end of assignments are finalized. Principals/program directors should verbally share information with impacted EAs.
May 8 HR sends electronic letters confirming displacements, layoffs and end of assignments to impacted EAs.
May 10 HR sends Interview Pool details to all EC-12 EAs via email.
May 11-12 EAs interested in open positions should email building/program contacts to request interviews.
May 15-17 Interviews take place.
May 18 Principals/program directors make offers.
May 19-22 EAs who accept new positions must complete a Job Acceptance Form with the hiring principals/program directors. The form must be signed and returned as a scanned email attachment to erica_strand@rdale.org.
May 23 HR sends out a list of remaining EA positions to displaced EAs who do not yet have positions.
May 24-25 EAs who do not yet have positions will be called in seniority order by HR and asked to choose a position.
June 5 Open EA positions will be posted on the Career Opportunities link on the district website.
June 5 Assignments for the 2022-23 school year will be available on
Skyward/Employee Access.
