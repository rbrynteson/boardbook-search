Mission
The mission of Robbinsdale Area
Schools is to inspire and educate all learners to develop their unique potential and positively contribute to their community.

Safety and Support Update
As ICE and federal activity increases in our communities, Rdale is following clear protocols to protect student safety, dignity, and access to learning.
Questions should be directed to your school principal, and resources are available at rdale.org → Community → All Are
Welcome: Immigration Resources.
If a student needs to be absent for an extended period due to safety concerns, families can work with their principal on learning supports, including temporary e-learning options.

Debate Excellence Continues
The Robbinsdale Debate Team delivered an outstanding showing at the 2026 MSHSL
State Tournament, earning top finishes across multiple events.
Cooper senior Lily Plahn won the State
Championship in Congressional Debate -
her second straight state title (2025-2026).
Rdale debaters also earned multiple MDTA
All-State recognitions, reflecting excellence and leadership statewide.

Engineering With NASA
Armstrong and Cooper students recently presented their NASA HUNCH designs at
Dunwoody College - receiving feedback from NASA engineers, professors, and peers.
For the first time since Rdale adopted NASA
HUNCH, both Armstrong and Cooper teams participated in the Preliminary
Design Review ahead of the Critical Design
Review in February.
Last year, Rdale was named a NASA
HUNCH Center of Excellence.

Career Paths Explored
On Jan. 6, approximately 60 Rdale high school students visited the Twin Cities Auto
Show for career exploration.
Students learned about the wide variety of automotive and mechanical career opportunities available after graduation.

Future Healthcare Leaders
On Jan. 16, Rdale celebrated 32 Certified
Nursing Assistant (CNA) students at a pinning ceremony held at Hennepin
Technical College (HTC).
Through this program, juniors and seniors at Armstrong and Cooper earn high school and college credit while preparing for healthcare careers.
A CNA pinning ceremony is a meaningful tradition marking completion of training and entry into the nursing profession.

Monsters With Meaning
Recently, Neill first graders partnered with
Armstrong students on an SEL project inspired by How I Met My Monster.
After creating "emotion monsters,"
students received handmade replicas sewn by Armstrong's Family and Consumer
Science class - some paired with original stories written by high schoolers.

Middle School Magic
Plymouth Middle School students brought Beauty and the Beast Jr. to life through an incredible theatre production this month.
The performances highlighted the talent, teamwork, and dedication of the cast and crew-and created a joyful community event.

Students Saving Lives
Earlier this month, Cooper hosted its second blood drive of the year with the
American Red Cross, resulting in 32
successful donations-enough to potentially help 96 people.
National Honor Society students volunteered throughout the day to support the event.

Noble Gives Back
Before winter break, Noble Elementary partnered with PRISM-a Golden Valley nonprofit that supports families in need-by hosting a schoolwide food drive.
With a goal of collecting 300 items, Noble students and families showed incredible generosity and community spirit by donating more than 700 items.

Award-Winning Leadership
RSI Co-Principal Dr. Alejandra Estrada-Burt has been named the 2025 MESPA West
Suburban Division Leadership Award winner.
Her equity-driven leadership and commitment to dual language education continue to strengthen outcomes and belonging for multilingual learners.
