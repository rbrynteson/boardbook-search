ROBBINSDALE Minutes of Work Session

Area Schools The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools

*"Reviewed January 24, 2023

A Work Session of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools was held Monday, January 9, 2023. Complete agendas, reports, and presentations are available at the office and on our website. Work session summary minutes are not approved by the School Board.

Director(s) present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Sharon Brooks Green, Greta Evans-Becker, Kim Holmes, Caroline
Long, and John Vento; and David Engstrom, Superintendent. Director(s) absent: none.

Curriculum and Instruction Overview

John Groenke, Executive Director of Student Services and Bridget Hall and Matt Pletcher, Directors of Curriculum presented in regard to the following plans: Three Pillars, Strategic Plan, LETRS Training and Implementation, Blended
Learning/Modern Classrooms, Culturally and Linguistically Responsive Teaching (CLRT) and Mathematics. Questions and discussion followed.

Board Topics
A. School Board Committee Assignments - Chair Evans-Becker lead the discussion regarding 2023 committee assignments. The Board through the committees to determine who would be acting and/or alternate representatives on each of them.

B. Proposed Ad Hoc Committee - Board Driven Outreach to Improve and Expand Communication - Director Bassett opened the discussion in regard to possible creation of this committee to improve interaction between the school board, community residents - with special emphasis on aligning segments of community outreach and communications efforts with our students and designated affinity groups within the district.
