ITEM:
"Disney's The Little Mermaid Jr."
Naida Grussing-Neitzel, PMS Principal
PRESENTER:
Nina Jonson, Director
Student Participants
Tonight's Arts Showcase features six students from the PMS production of "Disney's the
Little Mermaid Jr.," under the direction of Nina Jonson:
● 6th Grade: Elyse Leach
● 7th Grade: Ellie Harding, Adella Jonson and Lucia Ray
● 8th Grade: Hannah Lundberg and Aria Osborn
Journey "Under the Sea" with spunky heroine Ariel and her friends in this timeless tail of adventure, friendship and tough choices. As their desire for independence conflicts with their familial expectations, both Ariel and Prince Eric learn the power and importance of speaking up for what you truly want.
Some further information regarding this production:
All of the shows will be performed in the Robbinsdale Middle School auditorium.
Tickets will be sold at the door (CASH ONLY): adults $6.00, students $4.00 and kids under 2 are free.
Performance schedule:
● January 25, 2024 at 7:00 p.m.
● January 26, 2024 at 7:00 p.m.
● January 27, 2024 at 2:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m.
We hope you will enjoy their performance, and we appreciate them for joining us this evening.
