On January 18, 2024, competitive bids were received for the above-referenced project.
Of the eleven bids received, Park Construction Company submitted the lowest Base Bid in the amount of $457,637.

We have personal experience working with Park Construction Company and feel that they are competent and qualified to perform the specified work.
Based on the above information, we recommend that Park Construction Company be awarded the 2024 Tennis Court Rehabilitation project at Armstrong High School and
Cooper High School.
