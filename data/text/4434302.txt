ROBBINSDALE

Area Schools

Minutes of Closed Session
The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools

*"Approved November 20, 2023

A Closed Session of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools, ISD 281, was held Monday, November 6,
2023, beginning at 10:35 p.m. in the Superintendent's Office at the Robbinsdale Area Schools Education
Service Center, 4148 Winnetka Avenue North, New Hope, Minnesota.

1. Call to Order and Roll Call
Chair Helen Bassett called the meeting to order at 10:35 p.m.

Directors present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Sharon E. Brooks, Dr. Greta Evans-Becker,
Kim Holmes, Caroline Long and John Vento

Directors absent: None

Other attendees: Marti Voight - Interim Superintendent, Amy O'Hern - Executive Director of
Human Resources and Ukee Dozier from School Management Services LLC

2. School Board

A. Closed Session pursuant of Minnesota Statute 13D.03, Labor Negotiations Strategy
Chair Helen Bassett announced that in accordance with Minnesota Statute 13D.03, the meeting is closed to the public for the purpose of labor negotiations strategy, due to a digital device malfunction on Monday, September 18, 2023. The School Board reconvened with the same agenda as the previous meeting to ensure that a record of the session was captured -
keeping with District Administrative Policy 205, the proceedings were recorded.

3. Adjourn
Meeting adjourned at 11:08 p.m.

School Board Clerk, Independent School District 281
