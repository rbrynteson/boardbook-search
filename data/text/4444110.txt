On March 14, 2024, competitive bids were received for the above-referenced project.
Of the eleven bids received, Northwest Asphalt, Inc. submitted the lowest Base Bid in the amount of $686,100, as well as the lowest combined bid for the Base Bid and
Alternate No. 1, totaling $712,700.

We have personal experience working with Northwest Asphalt, Inc. and feel that they are competent and qualified to perform the specified work.
Based on the above information, we recommend that Northwest Asphalt, Inc. be awarded the 2024 LTFM - Site Paving project at the School of Engineering and Arts.
