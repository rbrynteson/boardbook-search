AGENDA SECTION:
3.B. Sharing the Success: Robbinsdale Debate Teams'
ITEM:
Success at the Minnesota State High School League (MSHSL)
Tournament on January 14, 2023
DJ Brynteson, Debate Coach
PRESENTER:
The Board recognizes the accomplishments of the Cooper and Armstrong Debate
Teams and DJ Brynteson, Debate Coach of both teams, for another successful season.
Congratulations to Robbinsdale Cooper Senior Connor Brynteson on winning the 2023
Minnesota State High School League (MSHSL) State Tournament in Congressional
Debate. This marks back-to-back State Championship wins and a fourth consecutive trip. Connor was joined by Robbinsdale Armstrong Senior Harrison Mantsch in the final th round of debate who took 5 Place overall.
In Lincoln-Douglas debate, Robbinsdale Armstrong Senior Daniella Mints was a semi-finalists in their first trip to the state tournament.
During the tournament, All-State Debate awards were given to the top 8 students in each event based on their season long performance. Congratulations to Connor,
Daniella and Harrison for all being named All-State Debate. This was Connor's fourth consecutive All-State Debate, the first students in MN Debate history to be awarded four consecutive years.
Congratulations!
