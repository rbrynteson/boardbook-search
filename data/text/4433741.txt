Minutes of Work Session
The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools
**Reviewed November 20, 2023
A Work Session of the School Board of Robbinsdale_Area Schools (RAS) was held Monday, November 6, 2023 in the
Boardroom at the Robbinsdale Area Schools Education Service Center. Complete agendas, reports, and presentations are available at the office and on our website. A recording of the meeting can be found at:
Work session summary https://www,rdale org/discover/school-board under "Watch School Board Meeting Webcasts."

Director(s) present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Sharon E. Brooks, Dr. Greta Evans-Becker, Kim Holmes, Caroline
Long, and John Vento; and Marti Voight, Interim Superintendent. Director(s) absent: none.
School Board and Administration Roles and Responsibilities
Helen Bassett, School Board Chair and Ann Goering from Ratwik, Roszak & Maloney P.A.
Chair Bassett and Ms. Goering reviewed responsibilities of the School Board and district administration, discussion afterwards.
Staffing and Budget Timeline
Amy O'Hern, Executive Director of Human Resources and Virginia Verbrugge, Assistant Director of Finance
Ms. O'Hern and Ms. Verbrugge reviewed the staffing and budget timeline for SY25, followed by questions.
Student Safety in Schools
Marti Voight, Interim Superintendent
John Groenke, Executive Director of Student Services
Bo Powell, Director of Security
Toni Boyden, Director of Student Services
Building Administrators: Alejandra Estrada-Burt (RSIS), Naida Grussing-Neitzel (PMS), Erick Norby (AHS) and Shaunece
Smith (CHS)
Interim Superintendent Voight, Mr. Groenke and Mr. Powell and building administrators reviewed suspension data comparing 2022-2023 and 2023-2024 to date, providing information on protocols, strategies and measures. A robust, emotional conversation ensued.

Helen Bassett, School Board Chair
1.
Superintendent Search Update - Six proposals were received, Board will review individually then meet at a

Retaining Support for School Board Self-Evaluation - discussion regarding past self-evaluation with MSBA (2022), and a consensus was reached to work with Deborah Keys Write to assist.
