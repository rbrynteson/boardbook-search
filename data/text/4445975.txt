School Board Norms
Drafted by the School Board during Homerun Leadership Professional Development
July 23, 2025
1. Board members will be prepared for meetings, and ask questions prior to the
Board meeting.
2. Abide by agenda for each meeting.
3. After a Board decision is made, speak with one voice and support the decision publicly.
4. Treat ALL individuals with courtesy, respect, and dignity - even when disagreement arises.
5. Accept and follow data practices.
6. We maintain a governance policy role, and support the superintendent in their management role.
7. Make requests for information only when necessary, and applicable to the work of the Board.
8. Be positive and mindful of how our actions in public and on social media reflect on the district.
9. Be student focused.
10. Respect each other's time.
