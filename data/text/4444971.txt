Agenda Item: 3
School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools
Business Meeting - December 2, 2024
Rdale Proud
AGENDA SECTION:
3. Showcase: Sonnesyn Elementary - Implementing Effective
ITEM:
Instructional Practices Bridge2Read and Catalyst
Mary Jane Adams, Principal of Sonnesyn Elementary
PRESENTED BY:
Ann St. Clair, Instructional Coach and EL Teacher
Marge Prohofsky, MTSS Coordinator
Michelle Azure, Assistant Principal
Alexandria (Ally) Adams, Current Center-Based EBD Teacher and Alum
PURPOSE:
Ms. Adams and her team from Sonnesyn Elementary will be sharing how Bridge2Read and Catalyst have provided teachers with effective instructional practices for teacher literacy and beyond. They will highlight both separately, as well as share how they have complemented each other. In addition, they will share the powerful role that coaching has played in their successful implementation, including short bits of video showing teachers in the classroom - so the Board can see what instruction looks like using these practices.
Alexandria (Ally) Adams, staff member and alum, will speak regarding her education in the district, and her pathway back to Rdale as a staff member.
ROLE OF THE BOARD:
Listen, and gain an understanding of how Bridge2Read and Catalyst are working over at Sonnesyn Elementary.
