PURPOSE:
The SEA team and students will be sharing information regarding their Annual
Pumpkin Drop Event.
Ms. Callen and Ms. Ewert are district alumni, and will speak regarding their stories within the presentation.
ROLE OF THE BOARD:
Listen, and gain an understanding of the Annual SEA Pumpkin Drop Event, and the paths of the two district alumni that led them back to working in the district.
Ms. Hanson, Ms. Hovde, Ms. Callen, Ms. Ewert and the students will present regarding the Annual Pumpkin Drop at SEA. It is a much-anticipated community event that brings together creativity, engineering, and a lot of excitement. Students design and build protective boxes to cushion a pumpkin as it's tossed from the roof of the school.
The goal is to see if their pumpkin can survive the drop without breaking. The students put their engineering skills to the test, considering materials, structure and design elements that will absorb the impact and protect the pumpkin. The event fosters a sense of community and teamwork as families, friends, teachers, and staff gather to cheer on each pumpkin's descent. It's a thrilling hands-on way for students to apply scientific principles, and a great time for everyone involved.
