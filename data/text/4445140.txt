COST SHARE AGREEMENT
BETWEEN THE CITY OF PLYMOUTH AND ISD 281
FOR IMPROVEMENTS TO
36TH AVE N AND PLYMOUTH MIDDLE SCHOOL

THIS AGREEMENT is made and entered into this _2 day of December, 2024, (the
"Effective Date") by and between the City of Plymouth, a Minnesota Municipal Corporation, 3400
Plymouth Boulevard, Plymouth, MN 55447, (the "City"), and the Independent School District 281, a
Minnesota school district, 4148 Winnetka Ave. N., New Hope, MN 55427 (the "District").

WHEREAS, the City is undertaking a public roadway improvement project along 36th Avenue including roadway repaving and the construction of additional medians and pedestrian infrastructure (the
"Project"); and,

WHEREAS, the Project includes improvements to 36th Avenue along the Plymouth Middle
School, 10011 36th Ave. N., Plymouth, MN 55441 (the "Middle School"), a school operated by the
District; and,

WHEREAS, in conjunction with and as part of the Project, the District desires to improve access and traffic flow to the Middle School, which will both reduce congestion and improve public safety; and,

WHEREAS, the District's proposed access and traffic improvements require the District to acquire additional right of way from adjacent properties; and,

WHEREAS, Minn. Stat. § 123B.74 authorizes the District to acquire the necessary right of way, including through the exercise of eminent domain if required; and,

WHEREAS, Minn. Stat. § § 471.59 authorizes the City and the District to enter into an agreement to cooperatively exercise any power common to the contracting parties, and one of the participating governmental units may exercise one of its powers on behalf of the other unit; and,

WHEREAS, the parties agree that the City shall cause construction of the Project.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises and covenants contained herein and subject to the provisions of Minn. Stat. § 471.59, the parties agree as follows:

1. Purpose. The parties enter into this Agreement for the purpose of completing the Project.
2. Term. Notwithstanding the date of the signatures of the parties, the term of this Agreement shall

commence on the Effective Date and shall terminate on the date that the Project has been completed, as determined by the City.

3. Duties of the City and District.

3.1 Solicitation and Award of Contract. The City has solicited bids for the construction of the Project based on the plans and specifications and has awarded a contract to the lowest responsible bidder, as determined by the City, in accordance with Minnesota law.

Plans and Specifications. The City shall prepare all plans, specifications, material quantities and all other documents necessary for the construction of the Project.

Construction Items. The City will let a contract and will cause the construction the
Project, in accordance with the District and City approved construction plans. The
Project includes, at a minimum, the following improvements, as further shown on the attached Exhibit A:

Construct median and pedestrian crossings along 36th Ave from west of the staff/bus lot east past the parent lot;

Construct traffic access point to the Middle School connecting to the intersection of 36th
Ave and Pilgrim Lane;

Construct concrete walk along 36th Ave.

Construction Oversight. During the Project, the City shall provide all necessary construction engineering, construction surveying, and construction inspection.

Right of Way and Permits. The City will be responsible for acquiring and paying for all land use rights necessary for the Project and shall be responsible for obtaining all necessary permits and other approvals necessary for the construction of or otherwise associated with the Project, such as all environmental permits including SWPPP, TEP
approvals, and NPDES permits if required. The District will grant the City Permanent
Easements along the entire northern border of the Middle School property Parcel ID 24-
118-22-11-0042, as well as Parcel ID 24-118-22-21-0001, to facilitate the Project, as shown on the attached Exhibit B. The District will also grant a Temporary Construction
Easements as shown on the attached Exhibit B. The City shall pay the District just compensation for these easements, as mutually agreed upon by the parties.

Administration. The City will be responsible for administering the Project. Contract administrative services shall include, without limitation, the advertising and acceptance of bids, generating partial and final payments and Project close-out. The City will also provide all necessary construction engineering, construction surveying, and construction inspection for the Project and will prepare and sign all work orders, change orders, and supplemental agreements relating to the Project.

Cost Participation. Each party shall be responsible for its share of the total project cost.
Relevant project costs shall be paid by each party in accordance with the construction quantity tabulation for items attributable to each party. Project costs include actual construction quantities and costs. Project costs also include fees incurred from the selected engineering consultant; each party agrees to pay a percentage of the engineering consultant fees that is equal to the percentage of actual construction quantities and costs attributable to that party. The salaries and wages of the City's and District's own employees are not Project costs; each party is solely responsible for paying its own employees. Attorney fees incurred in preparing, executing, and administering this
Agreement are likewise not Project Costs, and each party shall be responsible for its own attorney fees.

Payments to Contractor. For purposes of paying all contractors during the Project, the
City will process all payment requests from the contractors and will approve or deny the

requests as appropriate, pursuant to the terms of the contract.

Payments to the City. The District shall reimburse the City for its share of the cost participation, as defined above, as Project costs are incurred. The City shall track construction quantity tabulations, and may submit an invoice to the District for its share of the cost not more often than once per month. The District shall pay all invoices presented within thirty (30) days of the date the invoice is sent. The District shall notify the City of any dispute as to the amount owed before the thirty (30) day deadline runs.
The District shall still pay any undisputed portion of the invoice within the original thirty
(30) days.

Special Assessments Not Affected. Nothing in this Agreement shall limit the ability of the City to recover a portion of its Project cost through a special assessment under Minn.
Stat. Chapter 429. Such a special assessment would be in addition to the District's share of the Project costs as apportioned above. Nothing in this Agreement shall be interpreted as a waiver by the District of any right to participate in or appeal from such a special assessment.

Ownership and Maintenance. Following completion of construction, the City will own the concrete walk, median, and pedestrian crossings within the 36th Ave. right of way, and will be solely responsible for the ongoing maintenance and reconstruction of those improvements. The District will own the driveway access to the Middle School from the edge of the 36th Ave. right of way, including all interior roadways and pedestrian crossings within the Middle School property, as well as any other improvements constructed on District property outside of the City's permanent easements, and shall be solely responsible for the ongoing maintenance and reconstruction of those improvements.
