To: Board of Education
Re: Human Resources Update
Date: April 2, 2024
The staffing update will look different this week. I want to explain why our hiring for contracted teaching positions ends on February 12 and permanent education assistants ends on March 1.
State statute 122A.41 states that a teacher must complete at least 90 days of teaching service each year during the probationary period. Therefore if a district hires a teacher after February 12 they will not be contracted for at least 90 days and their first year of teaching would not count towards their probationary period. The February 12 date will change each year based on the school calendar and the number of contracted teaching days.
The rationale for not hiring permanent educational assistants is different. All full-time and some part-time employees of Robbinsdale Area Schools are required to carry health insurance. The district contributes to an employee's insurance premiums each month.
The insurance premiums for the months of June, July and August are allocated throughout the school calendar year. Therefore if an educational assistant is hired after
March 1 and chooses to take family insurance benefits their salary will not cover the insurance costs after their cafeteria allocation for the summer months.
