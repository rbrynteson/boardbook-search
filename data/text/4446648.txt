Memo
To: Robbinsdale Area Schools Board of Education, Superintendent Teri Staloch
From: Director of Facilities and Operations Maureen Mullen
Date: February 2, 2026
Subject: CHS, MLE, ENE Roofing Bid Award Recommendations
Background
The planned roof replacement cycle for Roddindale Area Schools is to replace roofs when their age reaches the 25-30 year mark, depending on roof condition and available funding. We wrapped up the last 30 year cycle in 2025, and begin a new planned roof replacement cycle in summer 2026 with small projects at three sites: Cooper High School, Meadow Lake Elementary
School, and Neill Elementary School.
Project details
The roofing sections being replaced this year are at the 26-28 years old mark. At all three sites, the building areas being reroofed are mechanical rooms, entries, and linking hallway spaces.
● CHS - Foyers over the west athletic entrances
● MLE - Mechanical rooms and linking areas
● ENE - Mechanical room
Current building code requires that the 2026 roof replacement projects include the following:
● Removing roofing materials and any wet insulation prior to re-roofing
● Installing new insulation to R-value 30 minimum where possible
● Creating a positive slope for roof drainage
● Constructing and installing an integrated metal fascia around the roof deck perimeter
● Conducting structural engineering to verify capacity for the added weight and R-values
Bid details
Roofing at the three sites were let as Base Bid #1 CHS, Base Bid #2 MLE, and Base Bid #3
ENE. Bids were received from 7 of the 7 companies invited to bid. Low bids were as follows:
● Base Bid #1 - $127,383 (JA Dalsin Roofing)
● Base Bid #2 - $356,353 (Commercial Roofing)
● Base Bid #3 - $96,000 (Berwald Roofing)
The planned LTFM budget for this project was $660,000. The total of $579,736 for the bids received is $80,264 under those estimated costs.
Considerations
These are some of the items reviewed when considering a recommendation:
● Maintaining the integrity of a building's roof is critical no matter what changes may be made to the interior of the building.

● District roofing project bids in 2024 ranged from $21 - $68 per square foot.
● District roofing project bids in 2025 ranged from $45 - $70 per square foot.
● This year's roofing project bids came in at $35 - $40 per square foot; understandably lower than last year as the scope of the 2026 projects do not include complete tear-offs.
Recommendations
For the reasons quantified above, I support the recommendations to award Base Bid #1 to JA
Dalsin Roofing, Base Bid #2 to Commercial Roofing, and Base Bid #3 to Berwald Roofing, and for the completion of all three roofs during the summer of 2026.
