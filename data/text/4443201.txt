MEMO
DATE: June 20, 2023
TO: Members of the School Board and Superintendent Engstrom
RE: Designation of Northwest Community Television - CCX Media as Educational
Television Producer for Events Conducted by the MSHSL
Robbinsdale Area Schools, ISD 281, designates Northwest Community Television -
CCX Media, of Brooklyn Park as its 2023-2024 educational television producer for events conducted by the Minnesota High School League when the district is unable to cover the events.
It is also understood that this designation gives clearance to Northwest Community
Television - CCX Media to cover regional and sectional events in which students from Robbinsdale Area School may be involved.
