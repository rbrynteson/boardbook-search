AGENDA SECTION:
6.L. Designation of Official Television Producer for 2026
ITEM:
Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent
COMMENTS BY:
Robbinsdale Area Schools, ISD 281, designates Northwest Community Television - CCX
Media, of Brooklyn Park as its 2026 educational television producer for events conducted by the Minnesota High School League when the district is unable to cover the events.
It is also understood that this designation gives clearance to Northwest Community
Television - CCX Media to cover regional and sectional events in which students from
Robbinsdale Area School may be involved.
Recommended Action:
Approve the designation of Northwest Community Television - CCX Media, of Brooklyn
Park as the district's official newspaper for 2026.
