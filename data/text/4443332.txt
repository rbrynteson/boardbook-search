To: School Board
Re: Contracted Services
Contracted services increased in the fiscal year 2021-2022 by almost 7.6 million compared to the fiscal year 2020-2021. Many of these services were not at normal expense amounts due to Covid-19
in the fiscal year 2020-2021 and the distance learning that was taking place for a good portion of the year.
Most of these services returned to levels or increased for the fiscal year 2021-2022. The amounts listed below are the areas that have the largest increases. Some areas did see smaller increases and others areas saw decreases from 2020-2021 to 2021-2022.
