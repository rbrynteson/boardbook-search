Appendix B
● Part of the Teachers
Contract
● Establishes the level of compensation for activities, but does not guarantee that the
District will offer a program or activity.

Parameters
Where more than one scheduled level of an activity is mandated by the Minnesota State High School
League, where at least two-thirds of the Classic Lake
Conference schools are offering that level, and where a coach is available, and where sufficient students exist to support each level, then such scheduled levels shall be provided by District 281 high schools, unless the specific level or entire program has been eliminated by the School Board.
