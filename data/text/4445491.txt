Statewide Funding Challenges

We Are Not Alone
Rdale's $21 million shortfall is part of a much larger trend across Minnesota.
● Association of Metropolitan School Districts
(AMSD) districts (50+) project $280M+ in shortfalls for 2025-26
● Rising costs + unfunded mandates are straining school budgets across Minnesota
● 23 districts will see current shortfalls grow because of cuts under the Governor's proposed budget
