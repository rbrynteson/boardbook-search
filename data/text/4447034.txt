AGENDA SECTION:
4. Growing through Nature and Outdoor Learning
ITEM:
Emilie Levinson, Director of Early Learning
PRESENTED BY:
Sara Tinklenberg, Early Learning Supervisor
Janet Rein, Preschool Teacher
Emily Maki, Early Childhood Special Education Teacher
Early Learning Students: Lucy Haug, Arthur Haug, and Onika
Schmitt-Lockridge
PURPOSE:
Ms. Levinson, Ms. Tinklenberg, Ms. Rein, and Ms. Maki will highlight the powerful role outdoor and nature-based learning experiences play in early childhood education.
The Early Learning team will share how outdoor experiences support young children's emotional well-being, encourage creativity, and foster important social skills such as cooperation, communication, and problem-solving. By connecting children with the natural world in developmentally appropriate ways, our Early Learning team creates rich learning experiences that nurture the whole child academically, socially, emotionally, and physically.
ROLE OF THE BOARD:
Listen, and ask questions.
