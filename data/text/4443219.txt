David Engstrom, Superintendent
From: Ukee Dozier, Executive Director of Finance
Date: June 20, 2023
Re: LTFM Revenue - Intermediate District 287 - Levy Pay 2024
RECOMMENDATION:
School Board approve by resolution, Intermediate District 287's Long-Term
Facility Maintenance Program and authorize the inclusion of the proportionate share of revenue in our application.
DISCUSSION:
The 2015 Legislature established a Long-Term Facility Maintenance Revenue (LTFMR)
program and allows Intermediate District participation provided approval is received from member districts. The Intermediate District 287 board approved their LTFM plan for the 2024-25
school year in the amount of $925,000 of which District No. 281's proportionate share is
$142,046.57, consisting of $22,724.38 pay as you go projects and $119,322.19 for debt service payments on the 2017B Facilities Maintenance Bonds ($5,065,000 issue) and the 2022A Facilities
Maintenance Bond ($4,750,000 issue). The member districts pay their proportionate share of bond payments and pay-as-you-go projects as indicated on the LTFM Coop Allocation Form. Please see the attached documents for details.
