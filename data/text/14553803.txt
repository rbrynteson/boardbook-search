To: School Board Members and Superintendent Staloch
From: Anthony Williams, Executive Director Community Education, Athletics and Activities
Date: October 19, 2026
Re: Action: MSHSL Form A Grant Resolution
Anthony Williams will request approval of a resolution to support the Form A Grant Application to the Minnesota State High School League (MSHSL) Foundation. Foundation grants support
Minnesota's high school youth and help to offset costs for students who qualify for reduced activity fees.
Student activity data gathered from the 2025-2026 school year in response to the application showed 269 students from Armstrong High School and 403 students from Cooper High School received free/reduced lunch benefits resulting in reduced activity fees. The total counts represent an unduplicated number of students who participated in high school activities.
The MSHSL Foundation is a nonprofit association. The Foundation awards grants to assist, recognize, promote, and fund extra-curricular participation by high school students in athletic and fine arts programs. Grants are awarded based upon gender balance, school size, and geographic location to the extent possible.
Recommended action:
Requesting approval of resolution to support the Form A grant Application to the Minnesota State
High School Leagues (MSHSL) Foundation.
