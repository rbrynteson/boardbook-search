ITEM: 5.A. Delegation of Deputy Treasurer

COMMENTS BY: Newly-Elected School Board Chair

The law allows a board to combine and delegate the duties of the offices of clerk and treasurer to person(s) employed by the district in its business/finance office. A resolution is required if the duties of the clerk and treasurer are delegated.

Per the Robbinsdale School Board Bylaws, the Treasurer (or Deputy Treasurer as the
Treasurer's designee) shall:

Treasurer.

Make certain that all records pertaining to the receipt and disbursement of district funds are properly maintained.

Receive, hold in custody and expend all funds as directed by the School Board.
Perform all other duties imposed by Minnesota statutes and other such duties as may be assigned by the School Board or the Superintendent of schools.
