To: School Board Members and Superintendent Dr. Teri Staloch
From: Executive Cabinet
Date: March 2, 2026
Re: 2025-26 Calendar Adjustment/Packing Day
Overview
In order to meet the needs of staff and provide a packing day at the end of the school year for K-8 buildings that are closing/consolidating, we have reviewed current calendar days and hours against statutory requirements, and collaborated with the teacher's union to develop a potential solution.
● There is no statutory day requirement for Kindergarten, only a 850 hour requirement.There are 162
kindergarten days scheduled this year which equates to 970 hours.
● Grades 1-6 have a 165 day requirement and a 935 hour requirement. These grades are scheduled 166
days and have 996 hours based on those days.
● Grades 7 and 8 have a 165 day requirement and a 1020 hour requirement. These grades are scheduled
166 days and have 1023 hours based on those days. However, locally, 6th grade gets lumped with 7th and
8th because they are all part of the middle school.
● Grades 9-12 are not impacted with a need to pack for moving.
Solution
Designating one day for packing and moving preparation will not impact the statutorily required days or minutes for students in grades K-6. However, removing one student contact day from the 7th and 8th grade calendar will put those students three hours short of the 1020 hour requirement. Therefore, if we utilize the currently scheduled professional development (PD) on May 1, 2026, as an asynchronous blended learning day and PD day for middle school, we can then change the last day of the school year for grades K-8 to June 1,
2026 and designate June 2, 2026, as a packing and moving preparation day. The middle school schedule for
May 1 will be similar to the previous asynchronous blended learning day and PD day. We anticipate a total of
120 minutes for lesson prep, lessons posting by 9:30 a.m., and time for student check in. The remaining time in the day will be principal discretion for providing building level PD, meetings, and additional work time. The PD
day (non-student contact day) for elementary and high school staff will remain as planned.
Specifics
1. May 1, 2026 remains solely a PD day (non-student contact day) for staff except for middle school. Middle school students and staff (grades 6-8) will also engage in an asynchronous blended learning day.
2. June 1, 2026 will change to the last student contact day for the 2025-26 school year for grades K-8.
3. June 2, 2026 remains the last day for grades 9-11, however changes to a staff only day (K-8) for packing and moving preparation.
4. K-8 staff not directly impacted by the consolidations, and therefore not needing to pack, will be expected to report to work as a non-student contact day.
