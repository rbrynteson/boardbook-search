School Board Study (Work) Session
Anthony Williams

Purpose
1. Understand how activities are allocated in
APPENDIX B: Co-Curricular Activities.
2. Understand contractual parameters for reducing a co-curricular activity.
3. Review considerations and discuss impact.
4. Receive feedback from the School Board.

Appendix B
● Part of the Teachers Contract
● Establishes the level of compensation for activities, but does not guarantee that the District will offer a program or activity.

Parameters
Where more than one scheduled level of an activity is mandated by the Minnesota State High School League, where at least two-thirds of the Classic Lake Conference schools are offering that level, and where a coach is available, and where sufficient students exist to support each level, then such scheduled levels shall be provided by
District 281 high schools, unless the specific level or entire program has been eliminated by the School Board.
