Description:
Rivertree School is requesting entering into a cooperative sponsorship with Armstrong/Cooper
Girls Lacrosse, beginning in the 2025-2026 school year. Rivertree School is a small school in Crystal with 43 students and does not have an interscholastic athletic program. The Rivertree lacrosse players were recently dropped from their co-op with Osseo/Park Center and would add 4 players to the Armstrong/Cooper program.
