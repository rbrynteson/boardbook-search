AGENDA SECTION:
7.C. Action - Application for Athletic Cooperative between
ITEM:
Armstrong/Cooper Wings and Northwest Passage High
School for Boys Swimming
Anthony Williams, Executive Director of Community
COMMENTS BY:
Education, Athletics and Activities
AC WIngs Boys Swim began the 2023-2024 school year as a Co-op between Robbinsdale
Armstrong and Robbinsdale Cooper High Schools. We are requesting entering into an additional cooperative sponsorship for Boys Swim, adding Northwest Passage High
School (NPHS) beginning in the 2024-2025 school year.
Our current Boys AC Swim and Dive roster numbers from both Armstrong and Cooper
High Schools have decreased over the past few years. Last year, the Armstrong and
Cooper Co-op had 13 swimmers in which 7 were seniors and graduated in 2024. This year, for the 2024-2025 school year, we currently have 12 registrations with potentially 4 more interested in joining, giving us a potential total of 16.
Recommendation: The School Board approves the Cooperative Sponsorship Application for Boys Swim between Armstrong/Cooper Wings and Northwest Passage High School.
