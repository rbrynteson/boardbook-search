To: School Board
From: Amy O'Hern, Executive Director of Human Resources
Re: Level III Custodian Grievance
Date: January 5, 2026
The purpose of this memo is to inform the School Board of a Level III
disciplinary grievance filed by a custodian pursuant to the grievance procedure contained in the collective bargaining agreement between the
District and the custodial bargaining unit (the "CBA"), and to outline the
District's recommended next steps.
Consistent with that prior recommendation, administration again recommends that the Board decline involvement and allow the grievance to proceed directly to arbitration, if the Union elects to do so.
The District recently received the arbitrator's decision regarding the grievant's first grievance. The arbitrator denied the grievance and found that the District acted within the terms of the collective bargaining agreement.
Subsequent to the issuance of that decision, the Union has filed a second grievance on behalf of the same employee. This grievance raises separate allegations and will proceed through the contractual grievance process as required under the collective bargaining agreement. The filing of this grievance is not unusual, and the District is obligated to process it independently of the prior matter.
Therefore, consistent with past practice and to preserve the Board's neutral role, administration recommends that the School Board:
● Decline to conduct a Level III hearing; and
● Refer the grievance directly to the next step of the grievance procedure, which is arbitration, should the Union choose to pursue it.
This approach avoids unnecessary Board involvement in an active labor dispute, maintains consistency with prior guidance provided to the Board, and preserves the integrity of the arbitration process as the appropriate forum for resolving disputes of this nature.
