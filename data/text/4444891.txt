Director ________________________ introduced the following resolution and moved its adoption:
RESOLUTION CANVASSING RETURNS
OF VOTES OF SCHOOL DISTRICT SPECIAL ELECTION
BE IT RESOLVED by the School Board (the "Board") of Independent School District No. 281
(Robbinsdale Area Schools), Hennepin County, Minnesota (the "School District"), as follows:
1. It is hereby found, determined and declared that the special election of the voters of this school district held on November 5, 2024, was in all respects duly and legally called and held.
2. As specified in the attached Abstract and Return of Votes Cast, a total of 62,338 voters of the School District voted at said special election on the question of renewing the School District's capital project levy.
3. A total of 37,736 voters voting in favor of School District Ballot Question 1, and a total of
12,938 voters voting against the question, the question was approved.
4. The School District's Clerk (the "Clerk") is hereby directed to certify the results of the election to the Auditor/Treasurer of Hennepin County, Minnesota. The Clerk is further authorized and directed to provide written notice of the results of the election to the Commissioner of Education in a timely manner.
