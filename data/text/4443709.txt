Board Topics
Helen Bassett, School Board Chair
1. Superintendent Search Firm Presentations
The School Board received presentations from the following superintendent search firms:
e Ray and Associates e Minnesota School Boards Association (MSBA)
e Hazard, Young and Attea (HYA)
e School Exec Connect
At the conclusion of the presentation, the directors scored each firm, then shared their scores with each other.
Once the scores were tallied, the firms were ranked as follows:
1. MSBA (278 points)
2. Ray and Associates (277 points)
3. HYA (276 points)
4. School Exec Connect (217 points)
After reference checks are completed, the School Board will bring forward MSBA for a formal vote on Monday,
December 4, 2023 at the Business Meeting to be the firm to assist them in finding our new superintendent.
