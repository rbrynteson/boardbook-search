Key Details
Capital Projects Levy
● Defined in statute: 123B.63
● Can be used for construction, technology, telecommunications, software and systems, textbooks, library materials and information systems and safety/security
● Revenue is based on net tax capacity - It changes
○ 2015-2016 - $3.5 million
○ 2024-2025 - $6.4 million
