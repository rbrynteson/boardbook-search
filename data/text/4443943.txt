To: School Board Members and Interim Superintendent Marti Voight
From: John Groenke, Executive Director of Student Services
Date: February 20, 2024
Re: Resolution: Non Resident Student Enrollment Capacity Limits
2024-2025 Non-Resident Student Enrollment Capacity Limits
Whereas, Minnesota Statute 124D.03 provides that a board of education may, by resolution, limit the enrollment of nonresident pupils in its schools or programs to a number not less than one percent of the total enrollment at each grade level in the district, and
Whereas, Robbinsdale Area Schools enrolls the number of non resident students that meet or exceed one percent of the total enrollment at each grade level in the district,
Now, therefore, it resolved that the superintendent or designee may close a grade, building or program for nonresident students as necessary due to space limitations.
Now, be it further resolved that the superintendent or designee may also limit the nonresident elementary enrollment so that 24-25 class sizes remain within the following range.
Class Size Range K 1 2 3 4 5
Minimum Class Size 18 19 21 21 23 24
Maximum Class Size 25 26 28 28 30 31
