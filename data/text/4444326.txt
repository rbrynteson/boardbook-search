Mental Health
Why we picked this topic...
Research Question: What impact does attending Robbinsdale Area Schools have on student mental health?

Mental Health
Observations
● Social Groups appeared to be set. Some people were sitting alone.
● Students looked disengaged
● There were students that looked confused in class
● Visible "trends" being followed

Mental Health
Survey Data
● About ⅕ of students don't have an adult that they can go to for support.

Mental Health
