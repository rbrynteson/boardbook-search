To: Cabinet
Date: 8/14/2023
Re: Levy Certification

By September 30th of each year the school board is asked to certify the property tax levies to the home auditor. This year the deadline is October 2, 2023. For districts that have reached an agreement with their home auditor, this deadline may be delayed to October 9, 2023.

This process starts with the district entering information into the MDE levy certification website. The deadline for the state to use the information entered by the districts and have the levy limit runs to the districts is
September 8th.

MASBO and MDE hold a levy certification workshop that will be held on September 11th. The district needs this information to get the numbers that will be presented to the board for approval. This information will not be available to prepare a presentation for the school board at the first meeting in September.

November 24, 2023 is the deadline for county auditors to deliver by mail a notice of proposed property taxes.
This notice cannot be mailed earlier than November 10th.

Districts are required to discuss the payable 2024 and 2023-2024 fiscal year budget at a regularly scheduled school board meeting and allow the public to speak after November 24th. The meeting must take place at 6

p.m. or later and the district may adopt the final levy at the same meeting.

Schools must adopt their final property tax levy by December 28, 2023.
