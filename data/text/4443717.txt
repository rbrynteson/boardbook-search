To: School Board
From: Marti Voight, Interim Superintendent
Date: December 18, 2023
Re: School Board Mediation Update
On December 4, 2023 the School Board voted and approved working with Mediation
Center of Minnesota (MCM). MCM is not available to be at the Business Meeting tonight, however some of the members of the facilitation team will attend the January 8, 2023
Business Meeting. The School Board will be provided the resumes/curriculum vitae of each facilitator confidentially.
Prior to that, MCM has requested that the School Board directors each provide them with what expectations they have, and what deliverables they want of the facilitation for/from the assessment. Interim Superintendent Voight will follow up tomorrow morning with an email outlining where this information will need to be sent, no later than Thursday,
December 28, 2023 at 5:00 p.m.
Phase I of this process will be completing an assessment via one on one interviews with a facilitation team member and each individual School Board member, Executive Cabinet member and the Executive Assistant to the Superintendent and School Board. Once these have been completed, the facilitation team will compile the information they received and move forward with determining their next steps.
