District Apollo and Athena Award Winners
Four student-athletes - two from Armstrong High School and two from Cooper High
School - have been recognized for their hard work both in the classroom and on the court.
Caitlyn Osanai from Armstrong High School (AHS) and Kéira Snapko from Cooper High
School (CHS) have been selected as their school's 2024 Athena Award winners. The award is given annually to one senior female who has distinguished herself in athletics and academics.
Dayton Franke (AHS) and Kameron Fox (CHS) have been chosen as the 2024 Apollo Award recipients. The award is also given annually to a male athlete who has excelled in athletics and in the classroom. This is the first year the Apollo Award has been presented in
Robbinsdale Area Schools.
Both the Athena and Apollo awards recipients will be recognized at their school during the academic awards ceremony.
