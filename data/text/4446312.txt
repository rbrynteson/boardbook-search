6.A. Annual Support of Forms A Application to the Minnesota
ITEM:
State High School League (MSHSL) Foundation
Anthony Williams, Executive Director of Community
COMMENTS BY:
Education, Athletics and Activities
Mr. Williams requests approval of a resolution to support the Form A Grant Application to the Minnesota State High School League (MSHSL) Foundation. Foundation grants support Minnesota's high school youth and help to offset costs for students who qualify for reduced activity fees.
Student activity data gathered from the 2024-2025 school year in response to the application showed 653 students from Armstrong High School and 324 students from
Cooper High School received free/reduced lunch benefits resulting in reduced activity fees. The total counts represent an unduplicated number of students who participated in high school activities.
The MSHSL Foundation is a nonprofit association. The Foundation awards grants to assist, recognize, promote, and fund extra-curricular participation by high school students in athletic and fine arts programs. Grants are awarded based upon gender balance, school size, and geographic location to the extent possible.
Recommended Action: Approve the resolutions to support the Form A Grant
Application to the MSHSL Foundation.
