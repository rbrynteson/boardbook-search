ITEM:
for the Minnesota Department of Education (MDE) External

Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent
COMMENTS BY:
The MDE requires school districts to annually designate an IOwA to comply with State
Access Control Security Standard 1.0 which states that all user access rights to Minnesota state systems must be reviewed and recertified at least annually. The IOwA will assign job duties and authorize external user's access to MDE secure systems for their local education agency (LEA).
The Superintendent recommends the Board authorize the following individual to act as the IOwA for the Robbinsdale Public School District 0281-01:
● Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent
The Superintendent also recommends the Board authorize the following individual to act as the IOwA Proxy for the Robbinsdale Public School District 0281-01:
● Dr. Bob McDowell, Assistant Superintendent
Recommended Action:
Approve the designation of Dr. Staloch as District 0281 IOwA, and the designation of
Dr. Bob McDowell as District 0281 IOwA Proxy.
