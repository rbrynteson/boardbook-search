Date: August 17, 2026
Re: Brooklyn Bridge Alliance for Youth Joint Powers Agreement 2027-2031
The School Board is asked to renew the Brooklyn Bridge Alliance for Youth Joint Powers
Agreement for the next five years, effective January 1, 2027, through December 31, 2031.
Funding for participation in the Alliance is $7,604.00 per year for the five-year agreement.
The Brooklyn Bridge Alliance for Youth is a collaborative initiative between the cities of
Brooklyn Park and Brooklyn Center, the four school districts that serve the students in the two cities, North Hennepin Community College, Hennepin Technical College, and
Hennepin County. The Alliance works to increase access, resources and the quality of Out of School Time programs with the goal of increasing high school graduation rates and paths to college and career.
Recommended Motion:
Approve the resolution authorizing participation in the Brooklyn Bridge Alliance for Youth.
