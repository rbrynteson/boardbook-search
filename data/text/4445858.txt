Minutes of Business Meeting
The School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools
**Approved XXXX
A Business Meeting of the School Board of Robbinsdale Area Schools (RAS) was held Monday, June 16, 2025, beginning at 7:00 p.m. in the Boardroom at the Robbinsdale Area Schools Education Service Center (ESC). A recording of the meeting can be found at: https://www.rdale.org/discover/school-board under "Watch School Board Meeting
Webcasts."
Call to Order and Roll Call
Directors present: Helen Bassett, ReNae Bowman, Dr. Greta Evans-Becker, Aviva Hillenbrand, Kim Holmes, Caroline
Long, and Dr. Kenneth Wutoh; and Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent. Absent: none. There was a quorum, and the meeting was called to order.
Acceptance of the Agenda
MOTION: Director Holmes moved to approve the agenda, Director Hillenbrand seconded the motion.
Motion prevailed with a 7-0 voice vote.
Rdale Proud: Community Education - Full Service Community Schools (FSCS), Connecting Students and
Families to the School Community
Anthony Williams, Executive Director of Community Education, Athletics, and Activities
Lakia McGahee and Nieshea Smith, Full Service Coordinators
Mr. Williams, Ms. McGahee, and Ms. Smith shared the positive impact of the FSCS program on district students, families, and community over the past four years.
Superintendent's Report
Dr. Teri Staloch, Superintendent
Superintendent Staloch talked about the district's mission to inspire and educate all learners to develop their unique potential and positively contribute to their community, and how RAS is committed to ensuring every student graduates career, articulated trades and college ready. She provided an update on activities, which included a presentation highlighting:
● Celebrating our graduates with a Robbinsdale Graduation Recap video
● Summer planning via System Leaders Advance meeting and District Data Retreat
● Upcoming Reimagine Rdale: Vision 2030 Team day of building tours on June 17, 2025
Safety and Security Report
Maureen Mullen, Director of Facilities and Operations
Bo Powell, Director of Safety and Security
Joel Mehring, Assistant Director of Technology
Information was provided regarding projects in progress, designed to improve safety and security in Rdale schools.

J. Policy Committee Report
The Policy Committee brought forward the following policies for first read, with the recommendation that they would come for second read/action at the Monday, July7, 2025 Business Meeting:
● 701 Policy - Establishment and Adoption of School District Budget
● 702 Policy - Accounting
● 706 Policy - Acceptance of Gifts
● 533 Policy - Wellness*
The proposed changes to each of these policies will bring them into alignment with the Minnesota School Boards
Association (MSBA) model policies. Those in the 700 Series were reviewed by the Financial Advisory Council
(FAC), with their recommendations reviewed by the Policy Committee.
*533 Policy will be returned to the Policy Committee for further discussion, prior to bringing back for second reading.
