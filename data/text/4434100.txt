RE: 36" Ave. Project in Plymouth

The City of Plymouth and the Three Rivers Park District are rehabilitating a portion 36""
Avenue in 2025. In addition to reconstructing the street, a regional trail along the south side will be installed along with related pedestrian and vehicular safety improvements.

The District owns land along the south side of 36" Avenue across from Trenton Lane N.
(the parking lot by the Armstrong High School baseball and softball fields) referred to as
Parcel 6, and in front of Plymouth Middle School, referred to as Parcel 8.

With respect to both Parcels, the District owns the land to the middle of 36" Avenue; there is no platted right-of-way or easement of record for the street. Under Minnesota law, ifa municipality maintains a road for six years or more, the roadway is deemed dedicated to the public by statute. Minn. Stat. § 160.05. As a matter of law, therefore, the City has a dedicated easement for 36'" Avenue, but as part of this project they are seeking to formalize that easement across both parcels.

In addition, the City seeks a trail, drainage and utility easement encompassing both the existing sidewalk (five feet in width) and an additional seven feet to allow room for the new trail. Finally, the City requests a temporary construction easement (9.5 feet wide) on the south side of the trail to aid in construction and provide room for possible sloping. The permanent and temporary easements for both parcels are attached. I have no objections to them.

The City engaged an appraiser to determine the value of the permanent and temporary easements. For the High School, the appraised value is $49,750. For the Middle School, which has more linear footage along 36" Avenue, the appraised value is $126,950. The total for both is $176,700. In my opinion, these amounts are reasonable.

I recommend Board approval of the Permanent and Temporary Easements in favor of the City of Plymouth for Parcels 6 and 8 of the 36" Avenue Rehabilitation Project.
