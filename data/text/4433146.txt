AGENDA SECTION:
7.D. Action - Application for Athletic Cooperative for
ITEM:
Armstrong/Cooper/Rivertree Girls Lacrosse
Anthony Williams, Executive Director of Community
COMMENTS BY:
Education, Athletics and Activities
Armstrong and Cooper High Schools and Rivertree School are requesting entering into a cooperative sponsorship for Girls Lacrosse, beginning in the 2025-2026 school year.
Entering into a cooperative sponsorship will allow the high schools to field two solid teams that can compete at the level appropriate for our enrollment.
The Girls Lacrosse team will be managed by the Armstrong Activities Department and compete in the Northwest Suburban Conference, with the goal of adding Tri-Metro opponents as non-conference games.
Recommendation: The School Board approves the Cooperative Sponsorship Application for Armstrong/Cooper/Rivertree School Girls Lacrosse.
