Sharing the Success - "Gummy Music" at Noble Elementary
Michael Rieckenberg, Principal at Noble introduced Kirsten Griebenow, Music Specialist and Rebecca Powers, Media
Specialist with their students. They explained Gummy Music was a special collaborative project between music, media services and technology integration which allows students to learn to play music on computers using circuit boards with gummy worms as the conductor in lieu of having a keyboard lab. They brought additional units so the
Board could try it.
Student Achievement Update - Robbinsdale Academy (RA)
Patrick Burrage, Assistant Principal at RA
Sarah Gaustad, Teacher at RA - Highview
Mr. Burrage and Ms. Gaustad explained the three schools that make up RA:
● Robbinsdale Virtual Academy (RVA) which has full and part time students in middle and high school
● Care and Treatment which provides educational support to students while participating.
● Highview Alternative Learning Center (ALC) which serves about 450 students per year ages 16-21 - with 165 of them graduating this year with an Armstrong or Cooper diploma.
Three students spoke about their experiences in RA, followed by questions from the Board.
