To: Members of the School Board
From: Amy O'Hern, Executive Director of Human Resources
Date: June 17, 2024
Re: Tentative Agreement - Principals
RECOMMENDATION:
The district administration is recommending the approval of a two-year Principal Contract with effective dates of July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2025. The following items have been tentatively agreed upon.
Salary increase of 3.1% in year one and 4.5% in year two.
Increase 403b match from $2500 to $2900 in year two of the contract..
Increase elementary assistant principal days from 200 days to 205 days starting the 2024-2025
school year.
Decrease elementary principal days from 230 days to 225 days starting the 2024-2025 school year.
Remove $1000 annual phone allowance from the contract.
Remove $1000 professional development/technology stipend from the contract.
