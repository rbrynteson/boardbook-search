To: Members of the School Board
From: Dr. Bob McDowell, Assistant Superintendent
Date: June 1, 2026
Re: Extended Field Trip - International Travel Requiring School Board
Approval
RECOMMENDATION:
The School Board approve the Armstrong High School Extended Field Trip to Germany,
Hungary, Austria, and the Czech Republic July 22-August 4, 2027.
DISCUSSION:
Per policy 610 Field Trips, revised March of 2026, field trips outside the United States must be approved by the school board.
Armstrong High School has approximately 15-20 students, and at least two staff chaperones that will go on this trip. Traveling to Germany, Hungary, Austria, and the
Czech Republic will give students an opportunity to explore new places, experience different cultures, and gain lifelong skills including independence and confidence.
Outcomes:
● Students will develop an understanding of the profound differences of another culture including: customs, norms, geography, and architecture.
● Students will engage in personalized learning experiences that connect to students'
interests, fostering deeper understanding and critical thinking.
● Students will interact with new people, places and cultures, expanding their knowledge of the world.
● Culturally, students will have a guided tour in each of the cities. In Berlin some of the highlights include: the Brandenburg Gate, Kurfurstendamm, Remains of the Berlin wall. In Prague they will see St. Vitus Cathedral, Golden Lane, and Charles Bridge.
In Budapest some of the highlights are: Heroes' Square, Castle Hill, and Gallert Hill.
Vienna brings Ringstrasse, Parliament Building, and Neues Rathus. Guided walking tours and a boat ride on the Danube River are also a part of this amazing itinerary.
Students and their families are responsible for the trip fees at about $5309.00 each.
These students will share their experience after the trip at a School Board meeting in
September or October of 2027.
