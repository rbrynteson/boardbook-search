Purpose

Key Messages
● Preliminary certification is a component of the overall levy process
● Revisions to the levy are still being made which will likely change the total amount
● Certifying the "Maximum" allows for greater flexibility
● The school board will have one more chance to act on the 2025
property tax levy
➢ Dec. 2, 2024 - Truth in Taxation hearing
➢ Dec. 16, 2024 - Certification

Timelines
Sept. 16 Board adopts Preliminary Proposed 2024 Levy
Payable 2025
Sept. 30 Deadline for school board to certify proposed property tax levies to county auditor
Mid-November County mails property tax statements showing estimated Payable 2025 property taxes and meeting date/time/location for the final levy and budget discussions
