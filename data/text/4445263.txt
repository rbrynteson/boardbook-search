Our mission
The mission of
Robbinsdale Area Schools is to inspire and educate all learners to develop their unique potential and positively contribute to their community.

Black History Month
Overview: February is Black History Month, and Rdale is committed to honoring and celebrating Black history, culture, and achievements. Join us on Wednesday, Feb.
12, from 5:30-7:30 p.m. at Robbinsdale Middle School for a special community event.
Impact: This celebration brings students, families, and the community together to learn, reflect, and engage in meaningful conversations about Black history.
All are welcome: A free meal will be provided, and all are welcome to take part in this evening of education, connection, and celebration.

Planting the seeds of learning
Overview: The beautiful weather on Jan. 30
made it the perfect day to start a garden! Kids at
Neill Adventure Club worked together to fill planters with dirt and topsoil before planting carrots, lettuce, dill, chives, and marigolds.
Impact: Over the next few months, they will take turns checking on the plants and watering them, learning teamwork and responsibility along the way.

AHS students shine for NASA
Overview: AHS students attended the
NASA Human Research Program
International Biomedical Conference in
Austin, Texas, representing Rdale as a
NASA HUNCH Center of Excellence.
Achievements: They presented to NASA,
Axiom, the Air Force, and international space partners, receiving valuable feedback and recognition. They also tackled real-world challenges, toured the
NASA HUNCH Assembly Facility, and more!

State of the District Town Hall
Event: The Town Hall at Armstrong High
School on Jan. 23 drew over 90 community members. The discussion focused on the district's financial outlook and future planning.
Highlights: Interpreter services ensured accessibility. Two dozen speakers shared feedback.
Impact: The event reinforced the district's commitment to transparency.

Vision 2030 Team kicks off
Event: The first Reimagine Rdale: Vision
2030 Team meeting took place Jan. 29 and brought together over 46 amazing people.
Highlights: Members reviewed key data, shared priorities, and focused on student success and equity.
Impact: The event reinforced the district's commitment to transparency.

Reimagine Rdale:
Community Events
Community engagement is at the heart of Reimagine Rdale. In February and March, we're hosting eight district-wide events, including two virtual sessions. Four will focus on engaging our Indigenous, Pan African, Somali, and Latino communities. All meetings will go from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m.
● Feb. 11 - Plymouth Middle School
● Feb. 19 - Virtual
● Feb. 24 - Sandburg Middle School (Pan African Community)
● Feb. 26 - Robbinsdale Middle School (Somali Community Engagement)
● March 10 - FAIR Crystal (Indigenous Community Engagement)
● March 19 - Robbinsdale Spanish Immersion (Latino Community Engagement)
● March 24 - Virtual
● March 26 - Meadow Lake Elementary School
