PURPOSE:
Ms. Smith, Mr. Nelson, Dr. Mahan will provide information highlighting opportunities for students to exercise their voice at CHS, and show how they build student skills for them to use their voice in the future. We will also hear from Emily and Chad, students at CHS, on how exercising their voice affects their educational opportunities. Ms. Lieser will discuss her experiences growing up in, and path back to the district.
ROLE OF THE BOARD:
Listen, and gain an understanding of Cooper Student Voice and how it benefits the students at CHS.
