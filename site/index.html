Search tips

  • bond referendum — documents containing both words.
  • "roll call vote" — an exact phrase.
  • budget OR levy — either word.
  • transport* — prefix match (transportation, transported…).
  • budget -referendum — exclude a word. NOT works too.
  • budget -"superintendent board report" — exclude a whole phrase.
  • budget -title:"board report" — exclude only by document title, so documents that merely mention it are kept.

This is an independent, open-source index of public meeting records. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by BoardBook or the district. Documents are linked back to their original source; the originals are always authoritative.
